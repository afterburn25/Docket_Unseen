# Episode 1 — The Fake DEA Letter Case

## Working Title

**He Sent Prosecutors a Fake DEA Letter — Then the FBI Looked Closer**

Alternative thumbnail text used during planning:
**RETURN MY FAKE ID**

## Case

Subject: **Matthew John Sanguine**

Core hook:
After police seized a driver's license bearing Sanguine's photograph but another person's identity, prosecutors received a purported DEA letter claiming the identity was connected to confidential informant work and asking for the license back. Prosecutors became suspicious and referred the matter to the FBI.
