# Docket Unseen

**Tagline:** Real cases. Overlooked stories.

Docket Unseen is a narrated, faceless YouTube documentary channel focused on under-covered real criminal cases and investigations: identity theft, fraud, scams, heists, cold cases, unusual prosecutions, and "how they got caught" stories.

The channel's differentiator is **finding interesting real cases that have strong public documentation but little or no obvious dedicated YouTube coverage**, then turning them into polished 8–15 minute mini-documentaries.

## Creative Direction

- Documentary-first, not sensational or gore-focused.
- Strong cold open built around the case's most surprising fact.
- Clear distinction between proven facts, reported allegations, and reconstructions.
- Original narration, graphics, timelines, maps, motion, and editing.
- Use real public records / official source material whenever available.
- Reconstructed visuals must be labeled **RECONSTRUCTION**.
- Allegations not established by the final disposition must be labeled **REPORTED ALLEGATION**.
- Do not fabricate realistic government IDs, badges, seals, or official-looking documents.
- Avoid graphic imagery when it is not necessary to tell the story.

## Standard Episode Structure

1. Cold open / hook.
2. Short Docket Unseen branded intro.
3. Case setup and timeline.
4. Investigation and evidence.
5. Natural mid-video request to like and subscribe.
6. Main reveal / resolution.
7. Outcome / sentencing / current status.
8. Closing reflection.
9. A story-specific viewer question asking for comments.

Do **not** use spoken "Part 1 / Part 2 / Part 3" section labels in the finished video.

## Audio

Primary narration workflow:
- ElevenLabs
- Current narrator: **Anna — British Neutral Narration**
- Calm, investigative documentary delivery.
- Narration is the master timeline for editing.

Background music:
- Low, tense documentary bed.
- Keep music at a **consistent low volume** under narration.
- **Do not duck or lower the music every time narration starts.**
- Only use gentle fade-in at the beginning and fade-out at the end unless a deliberate scene-specific change is requested.

## Visual Style

- 1920x1080, 16:9.
- Dark charcoal / black backgrounds.
- Off-white paper and document textures.
- Red accents.
- Evidence folders, case boards, timelines, maps, court graphics, source callouts.
- Slow camera pushes, pans, parallax, document slides, evidence-board reveals, timeline motion, and restrained crossfades.
- Transitions should follow narration changes, not happen randomly.
- Real documents should remain readable and mostly stable.
- Avoid flashy transitions that undermine the investigative tone.

2. Read `README.md`.

5. Check `stories/STORY_PIPELINE.md` before proposing a new case.
6. Update the repository at the end of the session with decisions, progress, new sources, and next steps.
