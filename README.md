# Docket Unseen

**Working tagline:** Real cases. Dark truths.

Docket Unseen is a narrated, faceless true-crime documentary channel focused on **compelling murder investigations, serial killers, notorious killers, juvenile / child killers, child-victim homicide cases, cold cases, disappearances, multi-victim cases, and major unresolved investigations**.

The channel is **not limited to obscure or under-covered cases**. Well-known cases with major public attention are welcome when the story is especially compelling, disturbing, historically significant, or rich in investigative detail.

A major target style is the **Jennings 8 / Jeff Davis 8 type of case**: multiple victims, overlapping relationships, small-community dynamics, conflicting theories, possible investigative failures, disputed allegations, and unresolved questions.

## Core Content Priorities

Primary:
- murder cases;
- serial killers;
- notorious killers;
- **juvenile / child killers — minors who committed or were convicted of homicide**;
- **cases involving child victims**;
- cold cases;
- disappearances with strong homicide/investigative angles;
- family annihilators;
- multi-victim / possible serial patterns;
- unsolved clusters such as the Jennings 8 / Jeff Davis 8;
- major solved and unsolved homicide investigations;
- cases involving disputed evidence, investigative failures, conflicting theories, or institutional controversy;
- cases solved by forensic, digital, behavioral, or circumstantial evidence.

Secondary:
- unusual fraud;
- identity theft;
- scams;
- heists;
- major non-homicide investigations.

Popularity is **not** a reason to reject a case. High-profile cases may be stronger choices because viewers already recognize the subject and want deeper explanations.

## Creative Direction

- Documentary-first.
- Serious, tense, and compelling without becoming exploitative.
- Strong cold open built around the case's most powerful fact or unanswered question.
- Clear distinction between proven facts, allegations, disputed claims, theories, and reconstructions.
- Original narration, graphics, timelines, maps, motion, and editing.
- Use real public records, court material, police/public releases, archival photographs, and reliable reporting whenever legally usable.
- Reconstructed visuals must be labeled **RECONSTRUCTION**.
- Allegations not established by a verdict, plea, or reliable record must be identified as allegations.
- Do not state that law enforcement, public officials, suspects, or other people committed misconduct unless supported by reliable established evidence; attribute disputed claims and theories to their sources.
- Avoid graphic gore unless absolutely necessary to explain a factual point; do not use graphic victim imagery as clickbait.
- Cases involving child victims require especially respectful language and visuals.
- Cases involving juvenile offenders should clearly distinguish offender age at the time, juvenile/adult-court status, and what the record actually established.

## Standard Episode Structure

1. Exciting 20–30 second cold open.
2. Short Docket Unseen branded intro.
3. Victim / subject / community context.
4. Timeline of events.
5. Investigation and evidence.
6. Natural mid-video like / subscribe request.
7. Breakthrough, arrest, trial, confession, key theory, or investigative controversy.
8. Verdict / sentence / present status — or clear explanation that the case remains unresolved.
9. Closing reflection on the strongest unresolved question.
10. Story-specific comment question.

Do **not** use spoken "Part 1 / Part 2 / Part 3" labels in the finished video.

## Audio

Primary narration workflow:
- ElevenLabs
- Current narrator: **Anna — British Neutral Narration**
- Calm but dramatic investigative delivery.
- Narration is the master timeline for editing.

Background music:
- Low, tense documentary bed.
- Keep music at a **consistent low volume** under narration.
- **Do not duck or lower the music every time narration starts.**
- Intentional musical rises may be used only for major story beats.

## Visual Style

- 1920x1080, 16:9.
- Dark charcoal / black backgrounds.
- Off-white paper and document textures.
- Red accents.
- Evidence folders, case boards, timelines, maps, court graphics, police/public records, archival photos, source callouts.
- Strong narration-driven animation: document reveals, animated evidence links, timeline movement, map routes, highlighted testimony, date/location transitions, photo parallax, and restrained cinematic transitions.
- Simple zoom/pan alone does **not** count as sufficient animation.
- Real documents should remain readable.

## Brand Intro

The channel intro should be exciting and reusable:
- roughly 8 seconds for the reusable logo sting;
- typically preceded by a 20–22 second episode-specific cold open;
- stronger sound design, animated evidence/case-file motion, logo reveal;
- working tagline: **Real cases. Dark truths.**
- do not use **"Overlooked stories"** as the permanent tagline.
