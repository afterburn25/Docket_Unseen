# Jennings 8 — Vehicle & Transportation Evidence Matrix

## Purpose

Track all vehicle / transportation evidence separately from suspect theories.

A vehicle link can be:
- **CONFIRMED** — official/contemporaneous source;
- **WITNESS CLAIM** — attributed witness/family statement;
- **LATER INVESTIGATIVE REPORTING**;
- **DISPUTED / UNVERIFIED**.

A vehicle link is not proof of homicide.

# Kristen Lopez

## White Chevy Silverado

### Established vehicle transaction
Louisiana Board of Ethics Opinion 2007-489 establishes:
- Chief Investigator Warren Gary negotiated to buy a suspect's vehicle during questioning;
- bought it March 30, 2007;
- sold it April 20, 2007;
- ethics violations / $10,000 fine.

### Later homicide-evidence allegation
Oxygen/Brown reporting says:
- witnesses had seen Kristen riding in a **white Chevy Silverado** associated with Connie Siler before death;
- later inmate/witness allegations said the truck may have transported Kristen's body;
- witnesses alleged Gary cleaned/resold the vehicle.

Source:
https://www.oxygen.com/martinis-murder/murder-in-the-bayou-possible-law-enforcement-missteps-in-jennings-murders

Status:
- purchase/resale/ethics violation: **ESTABLISHED**;
- Kristen riding in truck: **WITNESS / LATER INVESTIGATIVE CLAIM**;
- truck used to transport body: **UNVERIFIED THEORY**;
- intentional evidence destruction: **UNPROVEN ALLEGATION**.

Need:
- VIN;
- title/registration;
- Connie Siler ownership record;
- original witness statements;
- any search/forensic testing;
- car-wash receipt/video if claimed;
- buyer after Gary;
- full chain of custody.

Analytical importance:
This is one of the most concrete vehicle/evidence controversies in the entire series.

5. Which yielded fibers/DNA?

8. Do phone records place victims near specific drivers?
9. Does one vehicle type fit access to all recovery locations?

# Current analytical take

The vehicle evidence is **fragmented**.

The strongest public vehicle facts are:
- Brittney left Family Dollar alone/on foot;
- Necole later entered an unidentified vehicle;
- Warren Gary unquestionably bought/resold a vehicle during an active investigation, but its murder-evidence significance remains disputed.

Vehicle records could be one of the fastest ways to turn the current case network into testable exclusions.
