# Jennings 8 — Associated Deaths & Violent Incidents

## Purpose

A major source of confusion in the Jennings 8 case is the number of **other deaths** in Jefferson Davis Parish that later became folded into the story.

This file separates:
- documented unsolved homicides;
- police-involved deaths;
- family/journalist theories of connection;
- deaths explicitly said by law enforcement to be unrelated;
- cases where connection remains unknown.

Do not label a death "part of the Jennings 8" unless law enforcement or strong evidence supports it.

# Sheila Comeaux

## Core facts

KPLC's 2009 review of unsolved Jefferson Davis Parish murders listed:
- **Sheila Comeaux**
- beaten February 14, 1998
- died March 19, 1999

Source:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/11172484/jeff-davis-parish-investigating-13-unsolved-murders/

Oxygen's 2019 summary of `Murder in the Bayou` says:
- Comeaux was found severely beaten in an abandoned building on Valentine's Day 1998;
- she survived for about a year before dying from her injuries;
- family believed her death could be connected to the later Jennings 8;
- family/documentary reporting alleged she had been working as a police informant during a drug operation.

Source:
https://www.oxygen.com/martinis-murder/murder-on-the-bayou-sheila-comeaux

### Evidentiary status

Established:
- violent assault;
- later death;
- homicide remained unsolved in 2009 review.

Unverified / family-journalistic theory:
- police-informant status;
- specific drug-bust circumstances;
- connection to the eight.

### Why it matters

Comeaux's case predates Loretta by years and is often cited as an earlier example of:
- a woman from the same social environment;
- alleged informant involvement;
- severe violence;
- unresolved case.

But timing alone does not make her "victim zero."

Need:
- original 1998 police report;
- medical/autopsy/death certificate;
- family interview;
- CI documentation;
- task-force reference.

# Steven Thomas Gunter

## Core facts

A surviving **70-page Louisiana State Police investigation report** for the June 9, 2007 shooting is publicly accessible in an uploaded archive.

The report states:
- Louisiana State Police West District Detectives were asked by Sheriff Ricky Edwards to investigate the police-involved shooting at **507 Bliss Street, Lake Arthur**;
- Lake Arthur Police initially responded to a disturbance involving Steven Gunter;
- officers/deputies said Gunter barricaded himself with a weapon;
- JDSO deputies deployed chemical agents;
- the State Police synopsis says Gunter fired multiple rounds and one round struck an officer in the arm;
- Deputy **Terrie Guillory** returned fire with a Remington 870 12-gauge shotgun;
- the coroner pronounced Gunter dead at 2:53 p.m.;
- the later death certificate listed cause as **shotgun wounds of the trunk, right arm, and left leg**;
- LSP seized Guillory's shotgun, clothing, scene evidence, and interviewed involved officers/witnesses;
- the report records Guillory's law-enforcement history and statement.

Archive lead:
https://www.scribd.com/document/465742999/Terrie-Guillory-2007-Shooting-pdf

Important:
This is a surviving copy of an LSP case report uploaded by Ethan Brown, not the State Police's current official-hosted copy. A direct LSP-certified copy remains preferable.

Published Louisiana Court of Appeal case:
**Gunter v. Jefferson Davis Parish**, 84 So.3d 705 (La. App. 3 Cir. 2012), No. 11-1018.

Official/public text is available through Justia:
https://law.justia.com/cases/louisiana/third-circuit-court-of-appeal/2012/ca-11-1018.html

The opinion confirms:
- Steven Gunter died June 9, 2007 in Lake Arthur;
- wrongful-death/survival litigation followed;
- defendants included Jefferson Davis Parish, Sheriff Ricky Edwards, Terrie Guillory, other sheriff's personnel, the Town of Lake Arthur, and local officers;
- the appellate court affirmed summary judgment in favor of the Town/Lake Arthur defendants whose liability was before it;
- the record included depositions/affidavits, police logs, and a video of the residence.

This is significantly stronger than relying only on later documentary summaries.

The appellate opinion establishes:
- Steven Gunter died June 9, 2007 in Lake Arthur;
- civil wrongful-death litigation followed;
- defendants included Jefferson Davis Parish, Sheriff Ricky Edwards, Terrie Guillory and other officers;
- the trial court granted summary judgment to defendants on claims involving Town of Lake Arthur officers; appellate court affirmed.

Official/public case text:
https://law.justia.com/cases/louisiana/third-circuit-court-of-appeal/2012/ca-11-1018.html

A surviving Louisiana State Police investigation copy, later uploaded publicly, describes:
- law-enforcement response to a reported armed/barricaded incident;
- Terrie Guillory as a commissioned JDSO deputy / parish jail warden;
- Gunter's death from shotgun wounds.

### PJI allegations

Promise of Justice Initiative's 2020 DOJ request alleges:
- Gunter was a close associate of people connected to Jennings 8;
- Terrie Guillory shot him;
- PJI questioned the law-enforcement response and forensic details.

Source:
https://promiseofjustice.org/news/2020/07/08/the-promise-of-justice-initiative-demands-launch-of-federal-investigation-into-violent-corrupt-jefferson-davis-parish-law-enforcement

### Evidentiary status

Established:
- police-involved death;
- LSP investigation;
- Gunter was armed with a .22-caliber rifle according to the LSP investigation;
- LSP says Gunter fired and an officer was wounded;
- Terrie Guillory fired the shotgun that delivered fatal wounds;
- coroner/death certificate records cause as shotgun wounds;
- civil litigation and appellate ruling followed;
- multiple law-enforcement agencies were involved.

Unresolved / disputed:
- whether Gunter's death had any relationship to the Jennings 8;
- whether later advocacy claims identify material contradictions in the LSP report or scene evidence;
- whether Gunter possessed information relevant to the eight.

The primary LSP report makes the shooting substantially less ambiguous than some later summaries: it documents an armed barricade, gunfire from Gunter, and an officer wounded. Any series segment must present those facts before discussing later allegations.

### Why it matters

This case may be relevant because:
- it involves Terrie Guillory, a recurring figure in Brown's broader network theory;
- it provides a substantial public legal record;
- it adds context to claims about police-involved deaths in the same social network.

Do not present it as a Jennings 8 murder.

# Lacie Fontenot

## Core facts

KPLC Jan./Feb. 2014 reported:
- 27-year-old **Lacie Fontenot** was found dead in a shallow ditch near West 6th and Commercial Streets in Lake Arthur;
- Lake Arthur Police identified her;
- Jefferson Davis Parish Sheriff's Office assisted;
- initial cause of death had not been determined.

Sources:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/24609114/body-found-in-lake-arthur-identified-at-lacie/
https://www.kplctv.com/story/24619789/no-ruling-yet-on-lacie-fontenots-death/

KPLC's coverage of AG Buddy Caldwell explicitly stated:
- Fontenot's death **had not been linked to the Jeff Davis 8**.

Source:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/24658058/attorney-general/

### Later connection claims

64 Parishes' review of Brown's book says:
- Fontenot had associations with people in the broader Jennings network;
- sheriff's office rejected a connection.

Status:
**DEATH IN SAME PARISH / OFFICIAL LINK TO EIGHT NOT ESTABLISHED.**

KPLC's Feb. 2014 Attorney General coverage explicitly said Fontenot's death had **not been linked** to the Jeff Davis 8:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/24658058/attorney-general/

This is the default factual position unless later official records say otherwise.

Need:
- final cause/manner of death;
- arrest/prosecution outcome if any;
- direct sheriff/Lake Arthur case status;
- exact social links Brown cited.

Do not call Fontenot "ninth victim."
