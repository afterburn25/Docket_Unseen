## Archive handling

For each scan recovered:
- save exact publication;
- page number;
- author;
- date;
- headline;
- quote only short excerpts as needed;
- extract factual points into the relevant dossier;
- compare with KPLC and court records;
- mark conflicts.

Do not treat Crime Junkie/podcasts that listed these sources as the factual source; obtain/use the original newspaper page.
