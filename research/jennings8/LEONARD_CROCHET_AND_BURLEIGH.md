# Jennings 8 — Leonard Crochet Shooting & Harvey "Bird Dog" Burleigh

## Purpose

This file tracks the April 2005 Leonard Crochet shooting and the later death of Harvey Burleigh.

These events are frequently presented online as the possible "origin" of the Jennings 8 murders. That is **not established**.

The appropriate question is:

> Did the Crochet shooting create a pool of witnesses and information that later became relevant to the Jeff Davis 8 — or has a later theory connected events that were never proven to share a motive?

---

# Leonard Crochet — April 20, 2005

## What is strongly documented

A 2020 civil-rights investigation request from the Promise of Justice Initiative states:
- on April 20, 2005, Louisiana Probation and Parole officer **John Briggs Becton** shot and killed Leonard Crochet during a drug raid;
- Jefferson Davis Parish Sheriff's Office personnel were present;
- Becton said Crochet failed to comply and moved his hands toward his belt;
- no weapon or other item was found near Crochet that investigators said could be construed as a weapon;
- a witness behind Crochet gave an account inconsistent with Becton's account.

PJI's source page:
https://promiseofjustice.org/news/2020/07/08/the-promise-of-justice-initiative-demands-launch-of-federal-investigation-into-violent-corrupt-jefferson-davis-parish-law-enforcement

Status:
**ADVOCACY ORGANIZATION SUMMARY BASED ON CITED RECORDS.**
We still need the underlying State Police shooting file and grand-jury/court record.

## Grand jury outcome

Later reporting states:
- a parish grand jury considered whether Becton committed negligent homicide;
- it returned a **no true bill**, meaning no criminal charge was approved.

This is consistently repeated in Brown-related reporting and later summaries.

Status:
**STRONGLY REPORTED, PRIMARY GRAND-JURY / DA RECORD STILL NEEDED.**

Important:
The grand-jury decision does not answer every factual dispute about the shooting; it establishes that no criminal charge resulted.

# Who was reportedly present

Brown's reporting and later summaries say people connected to the later Jeff Davis 8 network were at or around the raid.

The strongest repeatedly cited name is:
- **Kristen Gary Lopez**, later victim #3.

Other names commonly cited in Brown-related reporting:
- Alvin "Bootsy" Lewis;
- Harvey "Bird Dog" Burleigh;
- additional South Jennings associates.

Brown later told A&E he had become **less confident** in the stronger claim that most or all eight women were present.

Series rule:
- state only specifically verified witnesses;
- do not repeat "all eight were there";
- make Brown's later revision explicit.

---

# Possible motive theory

A later task-force witness quoted in Brown-related reporting allegedly said:
> the women were being killed because they had seen something they were not supposed to see.

Frankie Richard also later linked the killings to what women had seen/heard/knew.

These statements created the theory that:
- Crochet's shooting produced witnesses;
- people who knew details about the shooting or police/drug relationships later became targets.

Status:
**UNRESOLVED THEORY.**

No public finding currently establishes the Crochet shooting as motive for any Jeff Davis 8 death.

---

# Promise of Justice Initiative allegations

PJI's 2020 DOJ request went further, alleging:
- Crochet had complained of harassment/threats from law-enforcement agents before his death;
- the shooting belonged to a broader local pattern of police misconduct.

Important:
PJI is an advocacy organization making a case for federal intervention.

Use:
> "PJI alleged..."

Do not convert those allegations into neutral established fact without obtaining cited source records.

# Harvey "Bird Dog" Burleigh

## Role in Crochet event

Later Brown/64 Parishes reporting describes:
- Burleigh as a Jennings prescription-drug dealer;
- the April 2005 raid as taking place at his residence;
- Burleigh as a witness/participant in the broader social network around Crochet.

Status:
**JOURNALISTICALLY ESTABLISHED CONTEXT; RAID RECORD NEEDED.**

## Death

KPLC reported in 2009, citing a Jennings Daily News review of unsolved parish killings:
- **Harvey Leigh Burleigh** was murdered July 25, 2007;
- he was found stabbed inside his home;
- his murder was among Jefferson Davis Parish's unsolved killings.

Source:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/11172484/jeff-davis-parish-investigating-13-unsolved-murders/

Status:
**CONTEMPORANEOUS LOCAL REPORTING OF UNSOLVED HOMICIDE.**

## "Close to finding out" claim

Later Brown-related reporting says:
- Burleigh told Whitnei Dubois's brother Mike that he was close to finding out who killed Whitnei;
- Burleigh was later found stabbed to death.

Status:
**JOURNALISTIC / FAMILY-WITNESS CLAIM.**

Need:
- Mike Dubois original interview;
- police report;
- suspect/disposition in Burleigh homicide;
- whether Burleigh's murder is still officially unsolved;
- whether investigators ever linked his death to Jeff Davis 8.

Do **not** say Burleigh was killed to silence him without evidence.

---

# Why this thread matters

Even if the Crochet and Burleigh deaths are ultimately unrelated to the eight:
- they show a high-violence, high-overlap environment;
- several people connected to the same South Jennings network experienced violent deaths;
- later witnesses/journalists interpreted those deaths through a cover-up lens;
- the theory influenced public understanding of the case.

That makes it necessary to investigate, but not to endorse.
