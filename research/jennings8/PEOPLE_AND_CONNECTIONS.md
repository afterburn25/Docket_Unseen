## Documented / well-sourced connections

### Kristen Lopez ↔ Brittney Gary
- A&E describes Lopez and Gary as cousins.
- KPLC contemporaneous coverage identified Brittney as related to one of the earlier victims.
- Status: ESTABLISHED RELATIONSHIP; exact degree should be confirmed from family/records.

### Victim social-circle overlap
- KPLC repeatedly reported that the women traveled in the same circle.
- A&E/Brown reporting says many knew one another.
- Status: BROADLY ESTABLISHED, but individual pairwise links still need documentation.

### South Jennings / South Main area
- KPLC reported several victims frequented the South Main Street / Doyle Street area.
- Status: CONTEMPORANEOUS REPORTING.

## Frankie Richard

- A&E identifies him as a local pimp/drug dealer connected to several victims.
- Brown's reporting places him close to Kristen Lopez's and Whitnei Dubois's final days.
- A&E says Richard was arrested in connection with Lopez's murder but released for lack of evidence.
- Richard denied involvement.
- Status: PERSON OF INTEREST / JOURNALISTICALLY REPORTED CONNECTIONS. **Do not imply guilt.**
- Primary arrest/warrant records still needed.

## Law-enforcement / informant connections

### Victims as informants
- Ethan Brown told A&E that one of the strongest connections he found was that all eight had worked as police informants.
- This is a major investigative claim from Brown, **not yet logged here as an official finding for every victim**.
- Status: JOURNALISTIC CLAIM REQUIRING VICTIM-BY-VICTIM SOURCE CHECK.

### Laconia Brown ↔ Michael Janise
- Brown told A&E that Muggy Brown had told relatives she was working as an informant on murder cases for officer Michael Janise; Brown also reported that Janise found her body.
- Status: ATTRIBUTED JOURNALISTIC CLAIM; needs primary records/family-source verification.

## Leonard Crochet shooting

- Brown's investigation explored whether the April 2005 police shooting of Leonard Crochet intersected with the later victim/witness network.
- Brown later told A&E that claims that most/all of the women were present were less certain than he initially believed.
- Status: IMPORTANT THEORY CONTEXT, NOT ESTABLISHED MOTIVE.

## Patterson case

### Byron Chad Jones / Lawrence Nixon
- A&E reports the men were initially charged in Ernestine Patterson's murder; charges were later dropped.
- Status: DOCUMENTED PROSECUTION HISTORY, but primary charging/dismissal records still needed.
- Do not describe either as Patterson's killer.
