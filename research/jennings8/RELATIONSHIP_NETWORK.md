# Jennings 8 — Relationship Network Ledger

## Purpose

This file is the backbone for the visual relationship map in the series.

Every connection must be tagged by evidentiary strength:

- **OFFICIAL / CONTEMPORANEOUS**
- **FAMILY / WITNESS**
- **INVESTIGATIVE JOURNALISM**
- **DISPUTED / UNVERIFIED**
- **UNKNOWN**

Do not draw all connections with equal visual weight in the final animation.

# 4. Frankie Richard

## Documented / strongly reported connections
- A&E / Washington Post / Oxygen describe Richard as socially connected to many or all of the women.
- Richard acknowledged knowing them and denied killing them.
- Contemporaneous KPLC in 2007 said his name had surfaced in multiple early cases.
- Richard became a recurring person of interest.

Status:
**MAJOR PERSON OF INTEREST / SOCIAL HUB.**

## What is NOT established
- that Richard killed any of the eight;
- that his later death was connected to what he knew;
- that every victim worked for him.

Final visual:
Richard should be a large node with multiple **dashed** links unless primary records establish a specific relationship.

# 14. Common-offender vs multiple-offender tension

## Official 2008 position
Sheriff Ricky Edwards said:
- cases were being worked individually and collectively;
- evidence did not yet allow certainty all were linked;
- referred to "killer or killers."

Source:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/9550151/jennings-press-conference-on-mystery-murders/

## Official Oct. 2009 position
Task force said:
- all eight were being treated as murders unless proven otherwise;
- collective agency opinion was they **may have been committed by a common offender**;
- several suspects/persons of interest remained on radar;
- no single exclusive suspect;
- nearly 1,000 leads;
- ~500 interviews;
- >48,000 investigative hours;
- reward raised to $85,000.

Source:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/11399926/jeff-davis-task-force-holds-news-conference/

## Later Brown position
Brown argues:
- multiple offenders better explain separate prosecution histories and case-file evidence.

Status:
**CENTRAL DOCUMENTARY TENSION.**

# 15. Relationship-map visual language

Use:
- solid white line = documented relationship;
- solid red line = documented criminal/investigative relationship;
- dashed amber = reported witness/journalistic claim;
- dotted gray = unresolved theory;
- blue node = law enforcement;
- red node = victim;
- white node = witness/person of interest;
- gold node = institution/location.

Never present the final network diagram without an on-screen legend.

# 15A. FBI Orion data-mining network evidence

A 2013 peer-reviewed study coauthored by Ricky Edwards analyzed 172 task-force Orion Information Packages related to Necole.

Published findings:
- Kristen and Brittney investigative terms clustered closely;
- Laconia/Muggy's investigative material was textually different;
- records separated into distinct geographic/textual sub-networks;
- task force said results contained previously unknown potentially important clues.

See:
`ORION_DATA_MINING_STUDY.md`

This is stronger than simply saying "everyone knew everyone" because it comes from the task force's own narrative intelligence.

It still does not reveal the masked identities or prove one/multiple offenders.
