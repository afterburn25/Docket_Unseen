# Jennings 8 — Part 5 Research: Brittney Gary & Necole Jean Guillory

## Purpose

This is the pre-script dossier for victims seven and eight.

This phase is unusually important because:
- Brittney was only **17**, the youngest of the eight;
- her final public sighting is documented by surveillance;
- her family began searching quickly and ultimately found her body;
- her death pushed authorities to formalize a multi-agency investigation;
- Necole's disappearance/body discovery occurred in almost real time while the task force was interviewing the missing-person reporter;
- Necole's mother later said Necole repeatedly predicted she would not live long;
- later documentary reporting says Necole believed police were involved, a stronger claim that requires careful attribution;
- after Necole, the task force publicly issued a common-offender behavioral profile.

Do not write final narration until Brittney/Necole autopsy, witness, and suspect files are deeper.

# Brittney Ann Gary

## Official baseline

Jefferson Davis Parish Sheriff's Office:
- DOB: 08/23/1991
- Age at death: **17**
- Last seen: 11/02/2008
- Body found: 11/15/2008
- White female
- 5'0"
- 130 lb
- brown hair

Primary:
https://www.jdpso.org/hot-cases/brittney-gary

## Final confirmed public sighting

KPLC Nov. 11, 2008 reported:
- around **5:30 p.m. Sunday, Nov. 2** Brittney entered the Jennings Family Dollar;
- she purchased prepaid cell-phone minutes;
- surveillance showed her leaving on foot;
- Jennings Police Chief Johnny Lassiter said she appeared to be alone;
- police believed she was walking the approximately five blocks home.

Source:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/9326888/surveillance-shows-last-moments-before-jennings-teens-disappearance/

Status:
**CONTEMPORANEOUS POLICE / SURVEILLANCE REPORTING.**

This footage is a top archival target.

## Early missing-person response

KPLC Nov. 6 reported:
- four days had passed without contact;
- Detective Joshua Crochet confirmed Family Dollar surveillance showed Brittney alone at about 5:30 p.m.;
- at that early stage police said they did not yet suspect foul play;
- Brittney's mother Teresa Gary strongly feared otherwise because of the six prior deaths.

Source:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/9301809/jennings-mother-fears-worst-in-daughters-disappearance/

Status:
**CONTEMPORANEOUS.**

## Brittney had expressed concern for her safety

KPLC Nov. 16 reported:
- Brittney's mother said Brittney had expressed concern for her safety following the discovery of six earlier women's bodies;
- Brittney was related to an earlier victim and friends with several others.

Source:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/9357379/body-discovered-believed-to-be-missing-jennings-teen/

Status:
**CONTEMPORANEOUS FAMILY REPORT.**

This is distinct from later theories about who she feared.

## Search and discovery

KPLC Nov. 16:
- Brittney's mother organized her own search efforts;
- family/friends searched areas where previous victims had been found;
- approximately **12:30 p.m. Saturday, Nov. 15**, a family search-party member called 911 after locating a body;
- scene was about **half a mile south of LA 1126 on Keystone Road**;
- FBI, Louisiana State Police, Jefferson Davis and Acadia Parish Sheriff's Offices, Jennings Police, and coroner personnel collected evidence and statements;
- tattoos appeared consistent with Brittney.

KPLC Nov. 18:
- Deputy Coroner Dr. Charles Deese confirmed the body was Brittney.

Sources:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/9357379/body-discovered-believed-to-be-missing-jennings-teen/
https://www.kplctv.com/story/9367809/jennings-teen-identified-as-7th-victim/

Status:
**CONTEMPORANEOUS OFFICIAL-BASED REPORTING.**

## Cause of death

KPLC 2019 retrospective states:
- Brittney's death was ruled homicide;
- cause involved **asphyxiation and mixed drug intoxication**.

Source:
https://www.kplctv.com/2019/06/07/discovery-id-hosting-special-jeff-davis/

Status:
**LATER LOCAL RETROSPECTIVE.**
Obtain autopsy/coroner before final script.

## Why Brittney's timeline mattered

Sheriff Ricky Edwards told KPLC that the usable missing-person timeline made Brittney's case different because most earlier victims had not been promptly reported missing.

KPLC Nov. 18:
- Edwards urged families not to wait to report someone overdue;
- investigators believed Brittney's timeline/evidence could help the investigation.

Source:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/9374347/dont-be-another-victim-in-jeff-davis-parish/

Status:
**CONTEMPORANEOUS SHERIFF STATEMENT.**

## Relationship to Kristen Lopez

KPLC and later reporting identify Brittney as related to Kristen.

KPLC Nov. 16 says Brittney had a family relationship to one prior victim; later sources describe Kristen as Brittney's cousin / first cousin.

Need:
- family genealogy/statement confirming exact relationship.

## Danny Barry allegation

Later Ethan Brown / Showtime reporting says:
- a witness allegedly told investigators Brittney was seen getting into a vehicle driven by Danny Barry after the Family Dollar sighting;
- Barry later denied knowing Brittney;
- Barry is now deceased.

Source summary:
https://www.oxygen.com/martinis-murder/murder-in-the-bayou-who-are-the-jeff-davis-8-key-players

Status:
**LATER INVESTIGATIVE ALLEGATION.**

Docket Unseen currently lacks:
- original witness statement;
- Barry police interview;
- Barry identity/employment history;
- death certificate/cause;
- corroboration of vehicle sighting.

Do not narrate Barry as Brittney's abductor or killer.

## Additional alleged final communications

Later documentary transcripts/secondary retellings describe:
- Brittney making a brief phone call after buying minutes;
- Brown research claims involving local associates.

These details are not yet supported by a logged primary phone record.

Status:
**RESEARCH LEAD.**

## Required primary records

## Necole predicted she would die

KPLC Aug. 2010 quoted her mother Barbara Guillory:
> "She'd always tell us that she wouldn't live long, and she knew that. She wouldn't tell us nothing. But, she knew."

Source:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/12982823/one-year-since-the-death-of-necole-guillory/

Status:
**CONTEMPORANEOUS/LATER FAMILY STATEMENT.**

This is strong evidence that family remembers Necole anticipating danger.

It does **not** by itself establish who she feared or why.

## Stronger claim: Necole believed police were involved

Oxygen's summary of `Murder in the Bayou` says Barbara Guillory stated:
- Necole had been vocal about her belief that police were somehow involved in the deaths before she disappeared;
- Necole told her mother she would not be there for her upcoming birthday.

Source:
https://www.oxygen.com/martinis-murder/murder-in-the-bayou-who-are-the-jeff-davis-8-key-players

Status:
**FAMILY CLAIM IN LATER DOCUMENTARY.**

Required:
- original Showtime clip/transcript;
- determine whether Necole ever reported this belief to police/task force;
- identify any names/details she allegedly gave;
- seek corroborating family/friend statements.

Docket Unseen should phrase:
> "Her mother later said Necole believed police were somehow involved."

Not:
> "Necole knew police were killing the women."

## Task force response after Necole

Aug. 20, 2009 task-force release:
- explicitly called Necole another female **homicide victim**;
- appealed for people with information who had not come forward;
- issued behavioral characteristics of "the offender";
- said offender likely had significant ties to Jennings/Jefferson Davis Parish;
- suggested possible behavior changes after murders, including travel, schedule, drug/alcohol use, or vehicle cleaning/sale.

Source:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/10966767/body-identified-as-necole-guillory-of-jeff-davis-parish/

Status:
**OFFICIAL TASK FORCE RELEASE.**

This is the strongest public move toward a common-offender profile.

# Why Part 5 is the end of the victim sequence, not the end of the series

With Necole:
- eight connected women were dead;
- task force publicly used common-offender language;
- family fear was explicit;
- several deaths had official homicide rulings;
- multiple prior prosecutions had failed;
- no one had been convicted.

The series should pause after Necole and transition into the **network/evidence/institutional investigation**, not rush directly into theories.
