# Jennings 8 — Evidence Ledger

## Evidence rules

Nothing goes into narration as fact unless we know:
1. what the evidence is;
2. which victim/case it belongs to;
3. who documented it;
4. where the source is;
5. whether it is confirmed, disputed, missing, destroyed, or merely alleged.
