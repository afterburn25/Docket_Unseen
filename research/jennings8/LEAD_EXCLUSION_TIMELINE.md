# Jennings 8 — Lead Exclusion / Availability Timeline

## Purpose

This file tests whether a named public lead was **physically available** during a victim's disappearance/death window.

It is not a guilt chart.

### Status labels

- **HARD EXCLUSION** — a verified incarceration/death/other record makes involvement in that victim window impossible.
- **POSSIBLE EXCLUSION — RECORD NEEDED** — arrest/trial status exists, but continuous custody is not proven.
- **NO PUBLIC EXCLUSION FOUND** — current public record does not provide an alibi/custody exclusion.
- **NOT A NAMED HOMICIDE LEAD** — included only because the person appears in broader theory/institutional discussion.

A lack of exclusion is **not evidence of guilt**.

# What the exclusion timeline currently tells us

## 1. There is no public one-person exclusion solution
No named local person in the current public record is cleanly excluded from enough cases to prove/disprove the broader one-offender theory.

## 2. Jeffery Lee Guillory is the clearest elimination
His Jan. 25, 2008 incarceration rules him out for victims 5–8.

## 3. Richard's May 2007 custody does not exclude him from the first four
Whitnei was already dead before his documented May 14 detention.

The later 2007 release also means that known incarceration does not exclude him from the 2008–09 deaths.

Again: availability is not guilt.

## 4. Jones/Nixon may yield important exclusions
If the Patterson defendants were jailed continuously through parts of 2007, they could potentially be excluded from Kristen/Whitnei.

But **"awaiting trial" is not sufficient proof of custody**.

Their jail/bond history is now a priority record request.

## 5. Later-deceased people are not excluded from the eight
Richard (d. 2020) and Barry (reported d. 2010) died after the final 2009 victim.

Their later deaths provide no exclusion for the eight victim windows.
