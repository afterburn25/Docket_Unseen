# Jennings 8 — 2002 Jefferson Davis Parish Jail Corruption Case

## Why this matters

This is one of the strongest documented bridges between a future Jennings 8 victim and proven / formally investigated law-enforcement misconduct **before the murders began**.

The key fact is not a conspiracy theory:

> **Necole Jean Guillory was a witness in the 2002 Jefferson Davis Parish jail misconduct investigation.**

Associated Press later reviewed newly released state and FBI records and reported that Necole, then 19, had been interviewed about misconduct inside the parish jail.

This does **not** prove her 2009 death was caused by what she knew.

It does establish that her knowledge of local law-enforcement misconduct was more than later rumor.

- an internal / criminal investigation occurred;

- Phillips pleaded no contest to felony malfeasance;

### Alleged / not adjudicated as criminal sexual-assault convictions

# 5. Why Necole's role matters to the Jennings 8

Before this record was located, the strongest public narrative was:
- Necole believed police might be involved;
- Necole repeatedly said she would not live long.

The 2002 case adds a harder factual layer:

**Necole had personally participated as a witness in an earlier investigation of misconduct inside the same parish jail/law-enforcement system.**

That makes these questions legitimate:
- What exactly did Necole tell investigators in 2002?
- Which officers/deputies did she identify?
- Who knew she had cooperated?
- Was she later a confidential informant or witness in other matters?
- Did she maintain contact/conflict with any people from that case?
- Did the Jennings 8 task force know about her 2002 witness role?
- Was that history evaluated as a possible motive after her 2009 death?

It still does **not** prove:
- police killed Necole;
- the 2002 jail case caused her death;
- any officer from the jail case was involved in the eight deaths.

---

# 6. Connection to later family claims

Later family/documentary reporting says:
- Necole believed police were somehow involved in the Jennings deaths;
- she told family she would not live long.

The 2002 witness record makes those later claims more contextually significant because she had firsthand experience with a documented law-enforcement misconduct investigation.

But causation remains unproven.

The correct documentary phrasing is:

> "Necole's fear of law enforcement did not arise entirely in a vacuum. Years earlier, state and federal records show she had been a witness in an investigation of misconduct inside the parish jail."

Then:

> "What is not known is whether that earlier cooperation had anything to do with her death seven years later."

This new information strengthens:

Because the jail misconduct investigation produced actual charges/pleas.

Because she had a documented prior witness role.
