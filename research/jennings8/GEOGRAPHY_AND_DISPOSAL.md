# Jennings 8 — Geography & Disposal Pattern Matrix

## Purpose

Compare recovery geography and body-placement patterns without assuming that similarity proves a common offender.

The core question:

> Do the recovery sites show one offender adapting over time, multiple offenders using familiar local dumping areas, or simply shared geography around Jennings?

# Pattern 1 — First three in water

KPLC's 2008 recap states:
- Ernestine was found in a drainage canal off LA 102;
- Kristen was found in Petitjean Canal off LA 99;
- Whitnei's roadway recovery was notably different from the first three canal recoveries.

Source:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/8998071/womans-body-found-in-local-canal/

KPLC 2019 confirms Loretta was found in a canal off Highway 1126.

Source:
https://www.kplctv.com/2019/06/07/discovery-id-hosting-special-jeff-davis/

### Possible interpretations
- one offender initially preferred water concealment;
- several offenders used canals because they were familiar/rural dumping sites;
- water sites reflect convenience rather than signature.

Status:
**OBSERVED PATTERN, NOT OFFENDER IDENTIFICATION.**

---

# Pattern 2 — Whitnei marks a visible shift

KPLC May 2007:
- Whitnei found near Bobby Road and Earl Duhon Road;
- article explicitly notes the first three victims had been found in canals.

Source:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/6511993/body-identified-in-jeff-davis-parish/

Showtime/KPLC-related material also emphasizes that Whitnei was placed visibly on/near the roadway.

### Analytical importance
If one offender:
- disposal behavior may have changed before Whitnei.

If multiple offenders:
- the site difference may reflect a different killer/motive.

Need:
- exact scene photographs/report;
- whether body was moved postmortem;
- tire/vehicle evidence;
- visibility from roadway.

# Pattern 4 — Necole and the I-10 change

KPLC:
- road workers found Necole between I-10 mile markers 72 and 73 in Acadia Parish.

Source:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/10960900/a-body-is-found-off-of-i-10-in-acadia-parish/

The Aug. 2009 task-force press release explicitly said:
- the offender may have changed method due to increased public awareness;
- Necole was discarded off Interstate 10 in Acadia Parish;
- previous victims had been discarded beside smaller rural roads.

KPLC preserved the release:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/10966767/body-identified-as-necole-guillory-of-jeff-davis-parish/

### Meaning
This is not just later speculation: **the task force itself interpreted the location change behaviorally.**

Still:
- offender-profile interpretation is not proof of one offender;
- multiple offenders could also explain location differences.

# South Jennings social hub versus recovery sites

KPLC Aug. 2009:
- several victims frequented South Main / Doyle Street;
- Necole was last seen getting into a vehicle on Doyle Street;
- task force later suggested the offender may stop frequenting the same Jennings area due to publicity.

Source:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/10998478/jeff-davis-victims-said-to-frequent-south-main-street-in-jennings/

This creates a useful two-layer map:

### Layer A — social / disappearance zone
- South Main
- Doyle Street
- Boudreaux Inn / related locations
- homes / known social addresses

### Layer B — recovery zone
- canals / rural roads / I-10

The person who links both layers is more analytically important than someone connected only to one victim socially.

# Preliminary analytical take

## Supports common-offender theory
- all sites are within the broader Jennings / nearby Acadia region;
- task force believed offender had strong local familiarity;
- later location changes could reflect adaptation.

## Weakens a simplistic one-offender signature
- first three are water recoveries;
- later sites are road/field/ditch;
- Necole's interstate site is materially different;
- separate suspect/prosecution histories exist.

## Supports multiple-offender or mixed theory
- distinct disposal modes;
- dense local network may give multiple offenders access to similar rural areas.

## Bottom line
Geography supports **local familiarity** more strongly than it proves **one offender**.
