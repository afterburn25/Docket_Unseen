# Jennings 8 — Final 72-Hour Contact Timelines

## Purpose

Reconstruct each victim's final known movements and contacts using the strongest available sources.

This file is designed to expose:
- recurring people;
- recurring locations;
- recurring vehicles;
- gaps between confirmed and reported sightings;
- which "last seen" claims are primary versus later recollection.

Evidence labels:
- **OFFICIAL / CONTEMPORANEOUS**
- **FAMILY / WITNESS**
- **LATER INVESTIGATIVE REPORTING**
- **UNVERIFIED LEAD**

Do not collapse a later family/documentary statement into an official last-seen fact.

# Loretta Lynn Chaisson-Lewis

## Official frame
- Last seen: **May 17, 2005** — JDPSO.
- Found: **May 20, 2005**.

## Reported final-day contacts

### Frankie Richard
**FAMILY / WITNESS**
Later family/documentary reporting says Loretta's brother Chad Chaisson last saw her at a local gas station getting into a vehicle with Frankie Richard.

Oxygen summarizes that account:
https://www.oxygen.com/martinis-murder/where-is-murder-in-the-bayous-frankie-richard-today

### Boudreaux Inn / Jermaine "Stymie" Washington / Muggy / Necole
**LATER INVESTIGATIVE REPORTING**
Ethan Brown's book says that on May 17 Loretta:
- was seen entering Richard's vehicle at a Phillips 66;
- later used cocaine at the Boudreaux Inn with Jermaine "Stymie" Washington;
- was with women Brown identifies as Muggy Brown and Necole Guillory later that day.

This is potentially very important because it would put:
- Loretta;
- Muggy;
- Necole;
- Boudreaux Inn;
- Richard / Washington

inside the same last-day social network.

But Docket Unseen does **not** yet have the original police/witness statements supporting the full sequence.

Status:
**HIGH-VALUE LATER REPORTING — PRIMARY LAST-DAY RECORDS REQUIRED.**

## Missing timeline records
- exact time at Phillips 66;
- vehicle description / ownership;
- Boudreaux Inn witness statements;
- Richard interview;
- Washington interview;
- Muggy/Necole witness statements;
- phone records;
- exact missing report.

# Kristen Gary Lopez

## Official frame
- Last seen: **March 6, 2007** — JDPSO.
- Found: **March 18, 2007**.

## Missing-report timing

KPLC later confirms Tracee Chaisson was the person who reported Kristen missing.

A community chronology says Tracee reported her missing around March 15–16, after a substantial gap, but the exact official report date should be verified from the missing-person file.

## Tracee Chaisson
**OFFICIAL / CONTEMPORANEOUS**
KPLC May 24, 2007 says:
- Tracee reported Kristen missing;
- investigators believed Tracee knew where Kristen's body was when she made the report;
- Tracee was booked as accessory after the fact.

Source:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/6565309/another-arrest-linked-to-the-death-of-a-jennings-woman/

## Frankie Richard / Hannah Conner
**OFFICIAL / CONTEMPORANEOUS INVESTIGATIVE LINK**
KPLC May 16–17 says:
- informant information led to Richard and Conner arrests in Kristen's case;
- both faced second-degree murder charges according to KPLC;
- Richard had been seen with at least three of the first four victims during their final days.

Sources:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/6526386/two-arrests-in-the-murder-of-kristen-gary-lopez/
https://www.kplctv.com/story/6528397/two-arrested-in-murder-of-kristen-gary-lopez/

What is still unknown:
- whether Richard or Conner were Kristen's actual final confirmed companions;
- exact last-seen address;
- exact time;
- vehicle;
- informant identity / statement.

## White Chevy Silverado
**DISPUTED / LATER INVESTIGATIVE REPORTING**
Witnesses later reportedly tied Kristen to a white Chevy Silverado associated with Connie Siler.

Ethics records prove Warren Gary later bought/resold a suspect's vehicle, but do not prove it carried Kristen.

## Missing records
- official last-seen witness;
- March 6 movements;
- missing report;
- Richard/Conner warrant affidavits;
- Tracee statement;
- truck witness statements;
- phone records;

# Whitnei Charlene Dubois

## Official frame
- Last seen: **May 10, 2007**.
- Found: **May 12, 2007** around the Bobby Road / Earl Duhon Road area.

## Family-derived final-night timeline
**FAMILY-DERIVED SECONDARY LEAD**

An older chronology based on Mike Dubois's account says:
- Whitnei spent time at her mother's / brother's home on May 10;
- left and later returned;
- Mike's mother woke him around 1–2 a.m. after Whitnei had returned and then left again;
- family discovered medication missing;
- Mike believed Whitnei walked to Frankie Richard's house one street over on McKinley Street;
- family heard nothing from Whitnei on May 11;
- authorities notified family May 12 that Whitnei had been found.

Source lead:
https://jdpkiller.wordpress.com/2011/09/27/3008/

Status:
**FAMILY-DERIVED SECONDARY LEAD.**

## Frankie Richard
**LATER INVESTIGATIVE / INDEPENDENT BROADCAST SUPPORT**

- KPLC May 2007 says Richard had been seen with three of the first four women during final days, but does not name which three.
- KNOE later reports Richard was last to see at least two victims alive.
- Ethan Brown specifically says Richard was one of the last people seen with Whitnei and that his alibis did not check out.

KNOE:
https://www.knoe.com/content/news/ArkLaMiss-Mysteries-Who-killed-the-Jeff-Davis-8-420659733.html

## Raw KPLC interview lead
The family chronology says Richard acknowledged in a KPLC interview that he saw Whitnei within hours of her death/discovery.

The raw KPLC Richard footage is therefore a **very high-priority archival recovery target**.

## Missing records
- official last-seen witness report;
- Mike Dubois original interview/audio;
- Richard police interview/alibi;
- raw KPLC Richard statement;
- phone records;
- vehicle/transport evidence;
- exact time-of-death estimate.
