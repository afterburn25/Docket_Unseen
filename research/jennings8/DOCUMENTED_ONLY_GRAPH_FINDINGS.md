# Jennings 8 — Documented-Only Graph Findings

## Purpose

This file records what remains when the case graph is filtered to higher-confidence evidence.

It is **not a centrality-based suspect ranking**.

Network degree can be misleading because:
- recovery-location edges inflate victim counts;
- exclusion edges can make an excluded person appear "central";
- institutions connect many cases by definition;
- an investigative relationship is not homicide evidence.

The useful question is:

> **Which broad relationship claims remain visible after speculative/later-reporting edges are removed?**

---

# Evidence filter

## A
Official / primary / contemporaneous official-based.

## B
Named family / firsthand witness.

The first pass removed C/D/E investigative-journalistic and theory-only edges.

# Main finding

## No single individual publicly connects strongly to most or all eight at A/B level

After stripping out later investigative-journalism edges, there is currently **no non-institution individual with documented positive victim relationships spanning most of the eight cases**.

This is important.

The popular narrative that one or two people sit at the center of every victim case relies heavily on:
- later interviews;
- Brown's case-file reporting;
- documentary claims;
- unrecovered task-force records.

That material may ultimately be accurate, but the public primary layer does not yet prove the full network.

# Investigative conclusion

The documented-only graph currently favors **caution**.

It does not show a clean publicly proven mastermind or unified offender network.

What it does show:
- Kristen has the richest public suspect/witness record;
- Necole has the richest public information/intelligence record;
- Patterson has a distinct failed prosecution;
- many of the most important cross-case relationships remain one evidence tier below primary verification.

That is exactly why the next major gains will come from the task-force report, court files, CI records, and DNA—not from adding more secondary theories.
