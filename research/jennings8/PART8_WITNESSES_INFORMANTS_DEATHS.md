# Jennings 8 — Part 8 Research: Fear, Witnesses, Informants & Later Deaths

## Purpose

This episode investigates the recurring claim that:
- several victims knew they were in danger;
- some had information about earlier murders;
- some had informant relationships;
- witnesses and connected people later died.

The episode must distinguish:
- documented fear statements;
- documented witness/informant activity;
- later journalism about task-force tips;
- deaths of connected people;
- unsupported "silencing" theories.

The phrase **"they knew too much"** is a hypothesis to test, not a conclusion.

---

# 1. Jesse Ewing and the inmate recordings

## Established event

KPLC contemporaneous reporting says:
- two female inmates requested to speak to Jennings Sgt. Jesse Ewing in December 2007;
- Ewing interviewed them separately;
- private investigator Kirk Menard said the two women told similar stories alleging a cover-up involving a **high-elected parish official**;
- Ewing gave the recordings to Menard;
- Menard delivered them to the FBI and Louisiana Attorney General's Office;
- State Police arrested Ewing for improperly releasing confidential investigative information;
- Ewing later pleaded to a reduced misdemeanor criminal-mischief charge.

Sources:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/7527604/officer-arrested-his-side-of-the-story/
https://www.kplctv.com/story/7520844/dec-19th-jennings-city-police-sgt-arrested/
https://www.kplctv.com/story/14878264/former-jennings-officers-running-for-jd-sheriff/

Status:
**TAPES/TRANSFER/ARREST ARE DOCUMENTED. COVER-UP CONTENT REMAINS ALLEGATION.**

Important:
Menard himself cautioned that he was not saying the cover-up claim was true.

# 2. Necole Guillory — fear before death

## Family statement

KPLC later quoted Barbara Guillory saying Necole repeatedly told family she would not live long.

Later documentary reporting says Barbara recalled Necole believing police were involved in the earlier deaths.

Status:
- fear/premonition: **DOCUMENTED FAMILY STATEMENT**;
- police-involvement belief: **LATER FAMILY/DOCUMENTARY CLAIM**.

## Alleged pre-death task-force warning

Pacific Standard's review of Ethan Brown's work reports:
- nearly one year before Necole's death, a witness told the task force that a client shared with Necole said Necole **"felt that she might be the next victim"**;
- Brown wrote that this tip received no follow-up.

Source:
https://psmag.com/news/murder-in-the-bayou/

Status:
**BROWN'S CASE-FILE-BASED JOURNALISTIC CLAIM.**

This is potentially one of the most important investigative-failure claims in the whole case.

Required:
- underlying task-force report;
- date of tip;
- witness identity;
- investigator assigned;
- follow-up log / absence of follow-up;
- whether the tip was ever re-opened after Necole's death.

Do not narrate "police ignored a warning" as settled fact until the underlying report is obtained.
