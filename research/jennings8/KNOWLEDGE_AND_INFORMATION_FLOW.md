# Jennings 8 — Knowledge / Information-Flow Matrix

## Purpose

This file tests the theory that some victims were targeted because they possessed information about:
- earlier murders;
- drug activity;
- law-enforcement conduct;
- informant relationships;
- people inside the South Jennings network.

The key question is not merely:
> "Were they afraid?"

It is:
> **What did each woman actually know, who knew that she knew it, and when?**

Evidence labels:
- **CONTEMPORANEOUS / OFFICIAL**
- **FAMILY / WITNESS**
- **INVESTIGATIVE JOURNALISM**
- **UNVERIFIED THEORY**

---

# Loretta Chaisson-Lewis

## Reported knowledge
**FAMILY / LATER REPORTING**
- Loretta's brother Nick Chaisson said she had told family she expected to be a witness / informant in a drug matter shortly before her death.
- Friend Jessica Kratzer later recalled Loretta acting frightened and giving her a purse to hide "just in case."

Source:
https://www.oxygen.com/martinis-murder/murder-on-the-bayou-sheila-comeaux

## Possible law-enforcement knowledge
**INVESTIGATIVE JOURNALISM**
- Ethan Brown's reporting links Loretta to the broader informant network.
- Brown later says Loretta had been around the area of the Leonard Crochet shooting, but he became less confident that most/all victims were witnesses to that event.

## Who may have known she was cooperating
Unknown publicly.

Need:
- CI file;
- drug-case witness list;
- handler/officer identity;
- exact threat or fear statement;
- police interview.

Status:
**POSSIBLE DRUG/INFORMANT KNOWLEDGE; NOT PRIMARY-VERIFIED.**

# Strongest "knew too much" chain currently

The public record is strongest for this sequence:

1. **Loretta dies.**
2. Brown says **Kristen was questioned about Loretta.**
3. **Ernestine dies.**
4. Brown says **Muggy was questioned about Ernestine** and a task-force witness claimed Muggy knew about Loretta's body.
5. Muggy later tells family she fears she is next.
6. Brittney, connected by family/social ties, expresses fear after six deaths.
7. Necole repeatedly predicts she will not live long.
8. Brown says a task-force tip before Necole's death said she feared she would be next.

This is a meaningful pattern.

It is **not yet proof** that information-sharing caused the killings.

---

# Key falsification questions

The theory becomes much stronger if primary records show:
- Kristen's Loretta interview existed and contained material information;
- Muggy's Patterson interview existed and contained material information;
- Loretta / Muggy / Crystal / others were documented CIs;
- Necole's pre-death warning is present in the task-force file;
- specific suspects/officers knew the women were cooperating;
- threats followed those interviews.

The theory becomes weaker if:
- interviews were routine/non-substantive;
- alleged CI status is unsupported;
- fear statements contain no specific threat;
- no common person had access to who was cooperating.

# Documentary rule

A fair line:

> "Several of the women were reportedly questioned about earlier deaths, and several later expressed fear before dying themselves."

A stronger line remains premature:

> "They were systematically murdered because they were informants."

The second statement requires evidence the current public record does not yet provide.
