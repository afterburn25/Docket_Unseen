# 2007 Kristen Lopez arrest record conflict

## Contemporaneous KPLC version

KPLC May 15–16, 2007 reported:
- Jefferson Davis Parish Sheriff's Office said Frankie Richard and Hannah Conner were arrested in connection with Kristen Gary Lopez's murder;
- Sheriff Ricky Edwards said informant tips gave investigators enough evidence for the arrests;
- KPLC reported Richard and Conner were facing second-degree murder charges for Lopez's death;
- Richard was already jailed on a separate rape allegation;
- sheriff said Richard had been seen with at least three of the four then-known victims in the days before their deaths;
- Richard had been interviewed in three of the four cases.

Sources:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/6526386/two-arrests-in-the-murder-of-kristen-gary-lopez/
https://www.kplctv.com/story/6528397/two-arrested-in-murder-of-kristen-gary-lopez/

Status:
**CONTEMPORANEOUS LOCAL REPORTING BASED ON SHERIFF'S OFFICE STATEMENTS.**

## Later Sheriff's Office version

Oxygen reported that, years later, the Jefferson Davis Parish Sheriff's Office told the `Dr. Oz` show:
- Richard had not been arrested for Lopez's death;
- he had been jailed on the separate rape charge;
- the rape charge was later dropped;
- Richard remained a person of interest and had not been ruled out of any of the eight cases.

Source:
https://www.oxygen.com/martinis-murder/where-is-murder-in-the-bayous-frankie-richard-today

Status:
**LATER OFFICIAL POSITION REPORTED SECONDHAND.**

## Required handling

This is a real public-record contradiction.

Before final scripting:
- obtain booking record;
- obtain second-degree-murder warrant/affidavit, if one exists;
- obtain jail booking ledger;
- obtain district-attorney charging record;
- obtain release/dismissal paperwork.

The episode should show the contradiction rather than silently choosing one version.

# Social connection to victims

KPLC 2007:
- Richard had been interviewed in three of the first four deaths;
- sheriff said he had been seen with at least three victims during their final days.

KPLC 2009 raw-interview article:
- said Richard knew most of the victims;
- investigators had initially called him a person of interest.

Washington Post / A&E / Oxygen later:
- describe Richard as knowing many/all eight;
- describe him as a pimp/drug dealer operating within the same South Jennings network;
- Brown says Richard was especially close to the final days of Kristen Lopez and Whitnei Dubois.

Status:
**STRONG SOCIAL / INVESTIGATIVE CONNECTION.**

What this does not establish:
- that Richard killed any victim.

---

# Richard's own denials

Richard repeatedly denied involvement in the murders.

Later documentary/reporting statements include:
- he said the women mattered to him;
- he denied killing them;
- he said the women died because they had seen/heard/knew something they were not supposed to know.

The last statement is highly important but ambiguous:
- it suggests Richard believed knowledge was motive;
- it does not identify who killed them;
- it does not establish his own innocence or guilt.

Use as:
**ATTRIBUTED STATEMENT FROM A PERSON OF INTEREST.**
