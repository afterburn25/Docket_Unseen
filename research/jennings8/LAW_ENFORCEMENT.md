# Jennings 8 — Law Enforcement / Investigation Dossier

## Multi-Agency Task Force

### Public formation
Sheriff Ricky Edwards publicly announced a full-time multi-agency investigative team on 12/18/2008 after seven women had been found dead.

KPLC preserved the press-conference statement.

### Agencies publicly identified in 2009 materials
KPLC's 08/20/2009 task-force release identified participation from:
- Acadia Parish Sheriff's Office
- Calcasieu Parish Sheriff's Office
- Jefferson Davis Parish Sheriff's Office
- Jennings Police Department
- 15th Judicial District Attorney's Office
- 31st Judicial District Attorney's Office
- Louisiana State Police
- Louisiana Attorney General's Office / Louisiana Department of Justice
- FBI

A later task-force news conference also named:
- FBI New Orleans Special Agent in Charge David Welker
- Louisiana State Police Captain Barry Branton
- Calcasieu Parish Sheriff Tony Mancuso
- Jennings Police Chief Johnny Lassiter
- Acadia Parish Sheriff Wayne Melancon
- Louisiana Department of Justice Director of Investigations Mike Thompson

Need full list of all 14 agencies referenced in later reporting.

## Ricky Edwards

- Jefferson Davis Parish sheriff during the core killings.
- Public statements evolved from caution about calling the deaths serial killings to acknowledgment that the cases appeared connected.
- KPLC 11/18/2008 quotes Edwards: he would not call them connected without forensic/scientific evidence usable in court, while saying he was "not a fool" to think they might be connected.
- Task-force messaging by Aug. 2009 used singular "offender" language.
- Research needed: term dates, full press conference archive, responses to later corruption allegations.

## Johnny Lassiter

- Jennings Police Chief in 2008–09.
- KPLC quoted him regarding Brittney Gary's disappearance and Necole Guillory's last known movements.
- On Necole, he said investigators believed the women had been with someone they did not fear.
- Research needed: full interview archive and later comments.

## FBI involvement

- KPLC reported FBI involvement as early as May 2007 in previous cases.
- FBI was formally named in the 2008–09 multi-agency task force.
- FBI FOIA logs show a 2022 FOIA request indexed under "Jennings 8/Jeff Davis 8"; this does **not** by itself establish new investigative activity.
