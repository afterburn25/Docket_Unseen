# Records-request strategy

Because these cases remain open, agencies may invoke investigative exemptions.

The best request is **not**:
> "Give me the whole active murder investigation."

Instead request:

1. the document/reports already referenced in a publicly filed federal civil-rights request by exact agency case number;
2. segregable, non-exempt portions;
3. public-facing / administrative sections;
4. final or historical witness reports where releasable;
5. an index / page count / record description if substantive pages are withheld;
6. the specific pages 46 and 104 if releasable;
7. final disposition / lead summaries that can legally be disclosed.

Ask each agency to identify the legal basis for withheld portions and provide reasonably segregable material.
