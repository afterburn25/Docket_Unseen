# The white Chevy Silverado / Warren Gary

## Ethics facts

Louisiana Board of Ethics Opinion 2007-489 establishes:
- on March 20, 2007 a suspect was questioned in an ongoing criminal investigation;
- Chief Criminal Investigator Warren Gary negotiated to buy her vehicle;
- he purchased it March 30 for $8,748.90;
- he sold it April 20 for $15,500;
- Board found ethics violations;
- Gary consented to findings;
- $10,000 fine.

The Ethics opinion **does not establish the vehicle was murder evidence.**

## Murder-evidence allegation

Brown/Oxygen reporting says:
- witnesses had allegedly seen Kristen in a white Chevy Silverado associated with Connie Siler before Kristen died;
- witnesses/inmate tapes later alleged the truck may have transported Kristen's body;
- one inmate alleged Gary bought it to dispose of evidence.

Source:
https://www.oxygen.com/martinis-murder/murder-in-the-bayou-possible-law-enforcement-missteps-in-jennings-murders

Status:
- vehicle transaction / ethics violation: **ESTABLISHED**;
- truck connection to Kristen: **REPORTED / DISPUTED INVESTIGATIVE CLAIM**;
- intentional evidence destruction: **UNPROVEN ALLEGATION**.

## Why this is central

Even without proving murder evidence was in the truck, the sequence creates a major conflict issue:
- lead criminal investigator negotiates private transaction during suspect questioning;
- purchases vehicle from person in custody;
- resells quickly for substantial profit;
- vehicle is later alleged by witnesses to be connected to a homicide investigation.

Docket Unseen should visually separate the ethics-board facts from later murder-related allegations.

# Timeline collision — May 2007

This two-week span should be animated carefully:

- **May 10:** Whitnei last seen.
- **May 12:** Whitnei found.
- **May 14:** KPLC reports identification; FBI assisting previous cases.
- **May 14/15:** Richard jailed on separate rape allegation.
- **May 15/16:** KPLC reports Richard and Conner arrests connected to Kristen.
- **May 16–17:** Sheriff publicly discusses informant tips and possible links to other victims.
- **May 24:** Tracee Chaisson booked accessory after fact in Kristen case.
- Patterson defendants were simultaneously reported as awaiting trial.

This was arguably the moment the cases stopped looking like isolated deaths and became a dense network of overlapping suspects, witnesses, victims, and investigations.
