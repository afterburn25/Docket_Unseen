# Jennings 8 — Necole Guillory's 2002 Jail-Corruption Witness Record

## Purpose

Necole Guillory's 2002 contact with law enforcement is an important **documented prior witness role**.

It may help explain:
- why she had firsthand knowledge of local jail/law-enforcement misconduct;
- why later family claims that she distrusted police deserve careful examination.

It does **not** prove that her 2009 death was caused by what she knew in 2002.

---

## Independent record-based confirmation

Associated Press reviewed newly released state and FBI records in 2019 and reported:
- Necole Guillory, then 19, had been a **witness in the 2002 Jefferson Davis Parish jail corruption case**;
- investigators interviewed her about misconduct and contraband inside the jail;
- the Showtime series included archival footage of Necole speaking with investigators;
- the broader 2002 investigation resulted in criminal charges against multiple jail officers.

## What PJI says about Necole's own statements

PJI says Necole:
- was interviewed by the Sheriff's Office;
- provided written statements;
- described misconduct/contraband inside the jail;
- had her information corroborated by correspondence among detainees.

This is an **advocacy filing's characterization of underlying investigative records**.

Docket Unseen should not reproduce sensitive personal details from the filing unless necessary to the story.

The important investigative fact is:
> **Necole had formally provided information about Jefferson Davis Parish jail misconduct years before the Jennings 8 killings concluded.**

---

## Why this materially changes the evidence map

Before finding these records, the "Necole knew too much" thread rested heavily on:
- her mother's later statements;
- Brown/Showtime reporting;
- Necole's fear before her death.

Now there is a separate, record-based fact:
- Necole had been a documented witness in an earlier law-enforcement corruption/misconduct investigation.

That gives her a **real prior knowledge relationship with law enforcement**.

It still does not tell us:
- whether anyone threatened her because of that 2002 case;
- whether the 2009 task force considered the 2002 case as a motive;
- whether the people involved in 2002 overlapped with her 2009 last days;
- whether any officer she discussed in 2002 was connected to the homicide investigation.

Good narration framing:
