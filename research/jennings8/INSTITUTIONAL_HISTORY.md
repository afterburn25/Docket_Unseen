# Jennings 8 — Jefferson Davis Parish Law-Enforcement Institutional History

## Purpose

Document older public-record history that may help explain community distrust of local law enforcement before and during the Jeff Davis 8 investigation.

**Important:** prior corruption, civil-rights litigation, ethics violations, or misconduct do not prove that law enforcement caused or covered up the Jennings 8 deaths.

---

## Dallas Cormier — Sheriff, 1980–1992

JDPSO's official history lists Dallas Cormier as sheriff from 1980 through 1992.

Official:
https://www.jdpso.org/history/dallas-cormier

### Federal criminal case

A 1994 Associated Press / Times-Picayune archive preserved in Ethan Brown's research reports:
- Cormier pleaded guilty to one federal count of obstruction of justice;
- he was sentenced March 29, 1993;
- sentence included supervised probation, community service, and a $10,000 fine;
- federal prosecutors dropped numerous additional charges as part of the plea arrangement.

Those dropped charges were allegations, not convictions.

Archive:
https://www.scribd.com/document/465739707/Dallas-Cormier-Federal-Indictment

## Fontenot & Brandenburg v. Cormier

Published federal appellate case:
**Fontenot v. Cormier, 56 F.3d 669 (5th Cir. 1995)**

Public text:
https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/56/669/624329/

The opinion establishes:
- a 1991 confrontation involving Jefferson Davis Parish deputies;
- one plaintiff had recently testified before a grand jury in a corruption investigation involving the Sheriff's Office;
- plaintiffs brought civil-rights claims against deputies and Sheriff Cormier;
- claims against Cormier were dismissed at trial;
- the jury initially found against several deputies;
- later qualified-immunity rulings were partly affirmed and partly reversed/remanded by the Fifth Circuit.

Why it matters:
This is a documented federal civil-rights record from the administration immediately preceding Sheriff Ricky Edwards.

What it does not establish:
- connection to the later Jennings 8 cases;
- wrongdoing by Edwards.

## Cormier public-records case

**Cormier v. Public Records Request of Di Giulio**, 553 So.2d 806 (La. 1989).

Public text:
https://hallapproved.com/la/cases/supreme/1989/1670733/

## Lisa Allen federal civil-rights complaint — 2007

Federal case:
**Allen v. Edwards et al., 2:07-cv-01675-CMH**
Western District of Louisiana
Filed Oct. 9, 2007.

A surviving copy of the complaint names:
- Sheriff Ricky Edwards;
- jail warden Terrie/Terry Guillory;
- jail employee Mark Ivory;
- District Attorney Michael Cassidy.

The complaint alleged serious civil-rights violations and failures to respond to reports of misconduct while Lisa Allen was incarcerated.

Surviving complaint:
https://www.scribd.com/document/465740452/Lisa-Allen-Sexual-Assault-Lawsuit

KPLC reported Oct. 10, 2007:
- the lawsuit had been filed;
- Sheriff Edwards said Louisiana State Police would investigate and the sheriff's office would cooperate;
- KPLC reported State Police had opened an investigation.

KPLC:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/7197004/jeff-davis-sheriffs-office-sued-over-sexual-assault-complaint/

## Why this belongs in the series

A fair statement is:

> Community distrust of Jefferson Davis Parish law enforcement had historical roots that predated the Jennings 8.

Supporting records can include:
- prior sheriff federal conviction;
- older corruption investigation;
- published civil-rights litigation;
- later ethics/evidence problems during the Jennings 8 era.

The series must also say:

> None of this proves that law enforcement killed the women or coordinated a murder cover-up.

The history is context for trust, conflicts, and institutional credibility—not proof of the murder theory.
