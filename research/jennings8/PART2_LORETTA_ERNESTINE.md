# Loretta Lynn Chaisson-Lewis

## Official baseline

Jefferson Davis Parish Sheriff's Office:
- full name: Loretta Lynn Chaisson Lewis;
- DOB: 12/26/1976;
- age at death: **28**;
- last seen: 05/17/2005;
- body found: 05/20/2005;
- white female;
- 5'4", 103 lb, brown hair.

Her father's message on the current sheriff's page says:
- she was a loving mother, wife, and daughter;
- the family asked anyone with information to come forward.

Primary:
https://www.jdpso.org/hot-cases/loretta-lynn-chaisson-lewis

## Discovery

KPLC's later retrospective reports:
- a fisherman found Loretta in a canal off Highway 1126 / Grand Marais drainage system;
- she had been seen alive three days earlier;
- decomposition was too advanced for authorities to determine a cause of death;
- drugs and alcohol were found in her system.

Sources:
https://www.kplctv.com/2019/06/07/discovery-id-hosting-special-jeff-davis/
https://www.kplctv.com/2019/05/21/fourteenth-anniversary-first-jeff-davis-body-discovered/

Important:
- later KPLC copy sometimes calls Loretta 29; current JDPSO DOB/age says 28.
- use **28** unless original coroner record says otherwise.

# Patterson prosecution history

## January 2006 — Byron Chad Jones arrest

KPLC reported on 01/20/2006:
- Byron Chad Jones, 32, had been apprehended in South Carolina on January 19;
- sheriff's officials said he was wanted in Ernestine's murder;
- KPLC said second-degree murder charges were pending forensic results;
- investigators said information received during the investigation led to Jones;
- sheriff's officials said they did not then believe Loretta's and Ernestine's deaths were connected, while not ruling it out.

Source:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/4390905/arrest-made-in-jennings-murder/

Status:
**CONTEMPORANEOUS LAW-ENFORCEMENT REPORT.**

## Lawrence Nixon

By May 2007 KPLC reported:
- Byron Chad Jones and Lawrence Nixon were both awaiting trial on **manslaughter charges** in Ernestine's death.

Source:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/6528397/two-arrested-in-murder-of-kristen-gary-lopez/

Important procedural discrepancy:
- Jan. 2006 article: Jones — second-degree murder charges "pending";
- May 2007 article: Jones and Nixon — awaiting trial on manslaughter;
- later coverage: charges dismissed.

A surviving older case-research page says it was summarizing court records and reports that investigators:
- conducted numerous interviews;
- took fiber samples from a vehicle trunk thought potentially to have transported Ernestine;
- removed/tested portions of a porch identified by a witness as a possible body-placement location;
- obtained no blood/DNA result tying those items to the killing;
- DA Michael Cassidy characterized the remaining case as dependent on an uncorroborated witness.

Source lead:
https://ernestinepatterson.wordpress.com/2008/12/15/13/

# Alleged Patterson crime scene and witness case

Brown/Oxygen reporting describes a much more specific theory:

- witnesses allegedly said Ernestine met Jones and Nixon while doing sex work;
- the group allegedly went to an abandoned house;
- Nixon's then-wife reportedly said the men later arrived with a large bag from which blood appeared to be leaking;
- Brown said the alleged house/porch evidence was not tested until about 16 months later;
- the later test failed to demonstrate blood.

Source:
https://www.oxygen.com/martinis-murder/murder-in-the-bayou-possible-law-enforcement-missteps-in-jennings-murders

Oxygen's key-player summary additionally says:
- witnesses implicated Jones/Nixon;
- Nixon's wife reportedly saw blood from a garbage bag;
- authorities ultimately concluded they lacked enough evidence to prosecute.

Source:
https://www.oxygen.com/martinis-murder/murder-in-the-bayou-who-are-the-jeff-davis-8-key-players

Status:
**BROWN / DOCUMENTARY ACCOUNT BASED ON CASE FILES; PRIMARY COURT/POLICE RECORD NEEDED.**

## Why the 16-month delay matters

If verified from the case file, delayed processing of an alleged murder scene is a central investigative-failure issue:
- biological evidence degrades/disappears;
- subsequent cleaning/weather/occupancy can contaminate a location;
- a negative test 16 months later does not answer what would have been found immediately after the crime.

Docket Unseen should not claim intentional sabotage without evidence.
