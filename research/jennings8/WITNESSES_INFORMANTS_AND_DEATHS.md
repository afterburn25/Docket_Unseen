# Jennings 8 — Witnesses, Informants & Later Deaths Ledger

## Purpose

A recurring claim in discussion of the Jennings 8 is that people who knew information, spoke to investigators, or were connected to the victims later died.

This file exists to test that claim **person by person**.

Do not use phrases like "everyone who came forward died" unless research can actually support them. At present, that statement is too broad.

For each person record:
- what they allegedly knew;
- whether they actually gave a statement;
- date/source of statement;
- whether they were an informant;
- later death date;
- official cause/manner of death;
- whether investigators linked the death to the Jennings 8;
- whether journalists/families theorized a connection.

## Frankie Richard

Role:
- longtime Jennings figure described in reporting as a pimp/drug dealer;
- connected socially to at least seven victims according to task-force/later reporting;
- person of interest in the Jeff Davis 8;
- repeatedly denied killing the women.

Important statement:
In the Showtime series / reporting, Richard said the women died because they had "seen," "heard," or "knew" something they were not supposed to know.

Death:
- died **March 22, 2020**, age 64.
- obituary confirms date of death.
- available reliable sources reviewed so far do **not** establish that his death was caused by, or connected to, his willingness to speak about the Jennings 8.

Status:
**DECEASED KEY PERSON OF INTEREST / SOURCE. CONNECTION OF DEATH TO CASE NOT ESTABLISHED.**

Research needed:

- whether later reporting's overdose/illness descriptions are supported by primary records;

## Series language rule

Acceptable:
- "Several people connected to the case later died, and some families and journalists have questioned whether particular deaths were related."
- "Brown argues that women who were questioned about earlier killings later became victims themselves."

Not acceptable without much stronger proof:
- "Everyone who talked died."
- "Witnesses were systematically killed."
- "Frankie Richard was killed because he was going to expose the truth."

Those stronger statements require direct evidence.
