# Jennings 8 — Boudreaux Inn Research Dossier

## Purpose

The Boudreaux Inn appears repeatedly in Ethan Brown's reconstruction of the Jennings social network.

The motel may be important as:
- a sex-work location;
- a drug/social-network hub;
- a place connected to Frankie Richard;
- a location connected to some victims;
- a later political controversy through Martin Guillory.

It must not be presented as the place "where all the murders started" without evidence.

---

# Location

Current/historical directory listings place the Boudreaux Inn at:
**15189 Louisiana Highway 26, Jennings, Louisiana 70546.**

Later Brown/Showtime material sometimes frames it geographically as part of the South Jennings / I-10 corridor.

Need:
- exact parcel;
- current status / demolition/shutter date;
- historic photographs;
- business-license history.

---

# Operation / lease history

Later reporting around Brown's book says:
- **Martin "Big G" Guillory** operated/rented or had an ownership/lease interest in the Boudreaux Inn;
- one later account says he ran it from the late 1990s through the end of 2004;
- Brown says he obtained a lease with Martin Guillory's name on it.

A 2026 Bayou Justice article reports a caller named Toby Leger said his company leased the Boudreaux Inn from Nov. 1999 to Jan. 2009 and that he went there weekly for payroll.

That newer claim needs independent documentation before use.

Status:
**MULTIPLE SECONDARY CLAIMS; PRIMARY LEASE/PROPERTY/BUSINESS RECORDS NEEDED.**

---

# Martin Guillory

Brown/Showtime:
- pulled a lease naming Martin Guillory;
- contacted Guillory about alleged criminal activity at the inn;
- Guillory denied knowing about criminal activity.

Later 2016 reporting:
- Guillory served as a field representative for U.S. Rep. Charles Boustany;
- Boustany said he did not know of Guillory's earlier motel connection;
- Brown's separate prostitution allegations involving Boustany were denied and did not allege murder involvement.

Status:
**BUSINESS/POLITICAL CONNECTION, MURDER LINK NOT ESTABLISHED.**

- Brown cited anonymous sources alleging Boustany had been a client of sex workers including some victims;
- Boustany denied the allegations;
- Boustany also said he did not know of Guillory's motel connection.
