# Jennings 8 — Necole Guillory Information-Exposure Timeline

## Purpose

Necole Guillory now has one of the strongest **documented information histories** among the eight women.

The key question is no longer simply:
> "Did Necole know something?"

There is public evidence that she had previously been a witness in a real law-enforcement misconduct investigation.

The more important questions are:
1. **What exactly did she report?**
2. **Who knew she had reported it?**
3. **Did that information remain relevant in 2009?**
4. **Did any later threat or homicide lead connect back to it?**

This file separates:
- documented information;
- documented exposure of that information;
- later family fear;
- unproven causal theories.

# What would turn this into a credible motive pathway?

At least one of the following:

1. A threat explicitly referencing her 2002 cooperation.
2. A 2009 task-force witness saying her prior jail testimony put her at risk.
3. Communication/contact between Necole and a 2002-case subject shortly before death.
4. A task-force motive memo linking the 2002 case to her homicide.
5. Her own statement identifying the 2002 case as why she was afraid.
6. Another victim sharing the same jail-case knowledge and a common threat.

None has been publicly established yet.

---

# What would weaken the theory?

1. Primary files show Necole was a minor/peripheral witness whose information was already public/resolved.
2. No relevant 2002 subject had later contact with her.
3. Her documented fear was directed at unrelated local people.
4. The 2009 task force examined the old case and eliminated it as motive.
5. A separate immediate offender/vehicle/forensic lead explains her death.

# Investigative conclusion — current evidence

We can now responsibly say:

> **Necole had documented firsthand knowledge of a prior Jefferson Davis Parish law-enforcement misconduct investigation.**

We can also say:

> **Years later, her family said she expected to die and suspected law enforcement in the women's deaths.**

We cannot yet responsibly say:

> **Necole was killed because of what she knew about the 2002 jail case.**

The missing seven-year causal bridge is exactly what the records requests must test.
