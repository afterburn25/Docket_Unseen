# Jennings 8 / Jeff Davis 8 — Master Timeline

**Research status:** Foundation pass started 2026-09-29.

This file contains only dates/events that have a cited source in `SOURCES.md`. Disputed material belongs in the theory/evidence files until verified.

## 2005

### April 20, 2005
**Leonard Crochet shot and killed during drug raid.**
- Later records/reporting identify Louisiana Probation and Parole officer John Briggs Becton as the shooter.
- PJI's 2020 federal-investigation request says no weapon was found near Crochet and cites a witness account conflicting with Becton's.
- Later reporting says a parish grand jury returned no true bill on negligent-homicide consideration.
- Status: SHOOTING IS ESTABLISHED; CROCHET-AS-MOTIVE THEORY IS UNRESOLVED.

### May 17, 2005
**Loretta Lynn Chaisson Lewis last seen.**
- JDPSO Hot Case page lists last seen: 05/17/2005.
- Status: OFFICIAL RECORD.

### May 20, 2005
**Loretta Lynn Chaisson Lewis found.**
- JDPSO lists date body found: 05/20/2005.
- Official DOB: 12/26/1976; age at death: 28.
- KPLC later described the location as the Grand Marais drainage canal off LA 1126.
- Status: OFFICIAL RECORD / contemporaneous local reporting.

### June 16, 2005
**Ernestine Daniels Patterson last seen.**
- JDPSO lists last seen: 06/16/2005.
- Status: OFFICIAL RECORD.

### June 18, 2005
**Ernestine Daniels Patterson found.**
- JDPSO lists body found: 06/18/2005.
- Age 30.
- KPLC later described her as found in a drainage canal off LA 102; later coverage states her throat had been cut.
- Status: OFFICIAL RECORD + local reporting.

## 2006

### January 19–20, 2006
**Byron Chad Jones arrested in connection with Ernestine Patterson's murder.**
- KPLC reported Jones was apprehended in South Carolina on January 19.
- Sheriff's officials said second-degree murder charges were pending forensic results.
- Investigators said they did not then believe Loretta's and Ernestine's deaths were connected, but did not rule it out.
- Status: CONTEMPORANEOUS LAW-ENFORCEMENT REPORT.

### 2006 — Lawrence Nixon also charged
- Later KPLC reporting identifies Lawrence Nixon as the second man charged in connection with Patterson.
- Exact arrest/charging date still requires court record.
- Status: LATER CONTEMPORANEOUS REPORTING.

## 2007

### May 2007 — Patterson prosecution status
- KPLC described Byron Chad Jones and Lawrence Nixon as awaiting trial on **manslaughter charges** in Patterson's death.
- This differs from the January 2006 report of second-degree murder charges pending against Jones.
- Obtain court docket before scripting procedural sequence.
- Status: CONTEMPORANEOUS REPORTING.

### March 6, 2007
**Kristen E. Gary Lopez last seen.**
- JDPSO lists last seen: 03/06/2007.
- Status: OFFICIAL RECORD.

### March 18, 2007
**Kristen E. Gary Lopez found.**
- JDPSO lists body found: 03/18/2007.
- Age 21.
- KPLC later described her as found by a fisherman in Petitjean Canal off LA 99.
- Status: OFFICIAL RECORD + local reporting.

### May 10, 2007
**Whitnei Charlene Dubois last seen.**
- JDPSO lists last seen: 05/10/2007.
- Status: OFFICIAL RECORD.

### May 12, 2007
**Whitnei Charlene Dubois found.**
- JDPSO lists body found: 05/12/2007.
- Age 26.
- KPLC reported her body near Bobby Road and Earl Duhon Road.
- Status: OFFICIAL RECORD + contemporaneous reporting.

### July 25, 2007
**Harvey Leigh "Bird Dog" Burleigh murdered.**
- KPLC's 2009 review of unsolved parish murders says Burleigh was found stabbed inside his home.
- Later Brown-related reporting ties Burleigh to the Crochet raid and says he had told Mike Dubois he was close to learning who killed Whitnei.
- No official link between Burleigh's murder and Jeff Davis 8 is established in the dossier.
- Status: UNSOLVED HOMICIDE + UNRESOLVED CONNECTION THEORY.

## 2008

### May 27, 2008
**Laconia Shontell "Muggy" Brown last seen.**
- JDPSO lists last seen: 05/27/2008.
- Status: OFFICIAL RECORD.

### May 29, 2008
**Laconia Shontell Brown found.**
- JDPSO lists body found: 05/29/2008.
- Age 23.
- KPLC later described her as found off East Racca Road by a city police officer.
- Status: OFFICIAL RECORD + local reporting.

### August 29, 2008
**Crystal Shay Benoit Zeno last seen.**
- JDPSO lists last seen: 08/29/2008.
- Status: OFFICIAL RECORD.

### September 11, 2008
**Crystal Shay Benoit Zeno found.**
- JDPSO lists body found: 09/11/2008.
- Age 24.
- Initial local reports described a decomposed body in a wooded area near a levee south of Jennings; later identification established Crystal.
- Status: OFFICIAL RECORD + local reporting.

### November 2, 2008
**Brittney Gary last seen.**
- JDPSO lists last seen: 11/02/2008.
- KPLC reported Family Dollar surveillance at about 5:30 p.m. showing Brittney buying prepaid cell-phone minutes and leaving alone.
- Status: OFFICIAL RECORD + contemporaneous surveillance reporting.

### November 15, 2008
**Brittney Gary found.**
- JDPSO lists body found: 11/15/2008.
- Age 17.
- KPLC reported the body was found by a family search party in a field on Keystone Road, nearly two weeks after her disappearance.
- Status: OFFICIAL RECORD + contemporaneous reporting.

### December 18, 2008
**Multi-agency investigative team publicly announced.**
- Sheriff Ricky Edwards announced a full-time team for the then-seven deaths.
- KPLC preserved the press-conference text.
- Status: OFFICIAL PUBLIC STATEMENT / contemporaneous reporting.

## 2009

### August 16, 2009
**Necole Jean Guillory last seen.**
- JDPSO lists last seen: 08/16/2009.
- KPLC reported witnesses said she was seen getting into a vehicle on Doyle Street.
- Status: OFFICIAL RECORD + contemporaneous reporting.

### August 19, 2009
**Necole Jean Guillory found.**
- JDPSO lists body found: 08/19/2009.
- Age 26.
- Her mother reported her missing to Jennings Police about an hour before a body was discovered along Interstate 10 in Acadia Parish.
- Status: OFFICIAL RECORD + contemporaneous reporting.

### August 20, 2009
**Task force issues public offender-behavior profile.**
- The multi-agency task force said the offender likely had significant ties to Jennings / Jefferson Davis Parish and might have changed transportation, routines, or substance use after the killings.
- The release was written in the singular ("the offender"), reflecting the task force's common-offender working theory at that time.
- Status: OFFICIAL TASK FORCE STATEMENT.

## 2010

### June 2010 — KPLC describes Patterson charges as dismissed
- KPLC reported Jones and the other Patterson defendant had had their charges dismissed.
- KPLC gave the official reason as lack of evidence and unreliable witnesses.
- Status: LOCAL REPORTING OF OFFICIAL RATIONALE.



### August 19, 2010
**One-year update after Necole Guillory's death.**
- KPLC reported law enforcement said the multi-agency task force was still working the cases and had made forensic progress it would not disclose publicly.
- Status: LAW-ENFORCEMENT PUBLIC STATEMENT.

## 2014–2019

### 2014
**Ethan Brown publishes major investigative reporting.**
- Brown's reporting later developed into the 2016 book `Murder in the Bayou`.
- Brown argues the single-serial-killer theory does not adequately explain the case.
- Status: JOURNALISTIC ANALYSIS, not official finding.

### 2019
**Major renewed media attention.**
- Investigation Discovery airs `Death in the Bayou: The Jennings 8`.
- Showtime airs `Murder in the Bayou`, based on Brown's reporting.
- Status: MEDIA / INVESTIGATIVE REPORTING.

## 2025–2026

### September 2025
**Ethan Brown revisits the case publicly.**
- `Buried Bylines` published an interview with Brown noting that the eight killings remained without justice two decades later.
- Status: MEDIA INTERVIEW.

### 2026
**Case remains publicly listed by JDPSO as eight Hot Cases.**
- JDPSO currently maintains Hot Case pages for all eight victims.
- A&E's Jeff Davis 8 feature was updated July 16, 2026 and continues to describe the murders as unsolved.
- Status: CURRENT PUBLIC RECORD / secondary reporting.
