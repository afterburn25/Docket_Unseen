# Jennings 8 — Part 4 Research: Laconia "Muggy" Brown & Crystal Shay Benoit Zeno

## Purpose

This is the pre-script research dossier for victims five and six.

This phase matters because:
- Muggy's death produced some of the strongest contemporaneous family statements that a victim feared she was next;
- later reporting says her throat had been cut;
- Crystal's death was formally ruled a homicide;
- Crystal was identified by DNA after significant decomposition;
- alleged informant connections become more prominent in later journalism;
- a later witness story involving Russell Carrier enters the "people who knew too much" theory but currently lacks sufficient primary corroboration.

Do not write final narration until autopsy/coroner records and the Russell Carrier record are deeper.

## Muggy feared she would be killed

KPLC June 1, 2008 interviewed older sister Kendra Brown.

Kendra said:
> "She knew her time had come."

The article explicitly says Muggy feared for her life days before her disappearance.

Jennings Police Chief Johnny Lassiter said at the time:
- investigators had no identified motive or suspect;
- several search warrants had been executed;
- several people had been questioned;
- police were following several leads.

Source:
https://www.kplctv.com/story/8407184/they-shouldnt-have-done-my-sister-like-this/

Status:
**CONTEMPORANEOUS FAMILY STATEMENT + POLICE STATEMENT.**

This is one of the strongest early pieces supporting a "victims were afraid" narrative because it was published immediately after Muggy's death, not years later.

**BROWN'S CASE-FILE-BASED JOURNALISTIC CLAIM.**

# Russell Carrier witness lead

## Ethan Brown / later retellings

A later secondary story repeated from Brown's investigative work says:
- after Crystal was found, a 43-year-old Lafayette man named Russell Carrier contacted the parish district attorney's office;
- Carrier allegedly said he saw three men leaving the woods near the recovery area;
- the named men were Eugene "Dog" Ivory, Ervin "Tyson" Mouton, and Ricardo "Tiger" Williams.

Later retellings say:
- Carrier died on October 10, 2010 after being struck by a train in Jennings;
- police reportedly described him as having lain on the tracks.

Current source trail:
- Ethan Brown's broader investigative work;
- podcast retellings.

At present Docket Unseen has **not** located:
- the original DA tip record;
- Carrier's written/interview statement;
- contemporaneous local reporting of his death;
- coroner death certificate;
- police incident report;
- any official finding that his death was related to Crystal or the Jeff Davis 8.

Status:
**HIGH-PRIORITY RESEARCH LEAD — NOT READY FOR FACTUAL NARRATION AS A MURDERED/SILENCED WITNESS.**

Series rule:
Do not describe Carrier as "killed for talking" or his train death as mysterious until records support the characterization.

# Sequence significance

By Crystal's identification in November 2008:
- six connected women had been found dead;
- at least three deaths were being treated as homicides according to contemporary reporting;
- Muggy's family had publicly said she feared for her life;
- authorities were comparing Crystal's evidence to previous cases;
- public concern about a serial offender was intensifying.

Then, before Crystal's identification was publicly confirmed, **17-year-old Brittney Gary disappeared**.

That makes the transition from Part 4 into Brittney's story especially strong.
