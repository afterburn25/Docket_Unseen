Official City Clerk page:
https://www.cityofjennings.com/city-clerks-office/

Official public-record request form:
https://www.cityofjennings.com/?mdocs-file=3643

Form instructions:
- submit to City of Jennings Public Records Coordinator;
- P.O. Box 1249, Jennings, LA 70546;
- email: **cityhall@cityofjennings.com**;

## Jefferson Davis Parish Clerk of Court

Official criminal/civil page:
https://www.jeffdavisclerk.org/civilCriminal

The Clerk states:
- Criminal Department files bills of information, indictments, criminal motions, subpoenas, notices;
- Criminal Minute Clerk maintains minutes and filed exhibits;
- copies of pleadings/minutes and criminal searches are available;
- criminal search: $20/name for ten years;
- copies: $2/page plus certification fee;
- criminal minute extracts: $15.

Contact:
- 300 N. State St., Room 106, Jennings, LA 70546
- Civil/Criminal/Probate: **337-824-8340**

Use for:
- Jones/Nixon Patterson prosecution;
- Richard/Conner/Tracee Lopez proceedings;
- Gunter civil case **C-488-08**;

## Strategy

Start with:
1. City of Jennings — K-06184-08.
2. LSP — exact case-number requests.
3. Clerk — named criminal searches / minutes.
4. JDPSO — public-facing/task-force documents.
5. FBI eFOIPA after local/state identifiers are better mapped.

The most valuable result may be a **case index / face sheet**, even when full investigative material remains exempt, because it can reveal cross-referenced case numbers and custodians.
