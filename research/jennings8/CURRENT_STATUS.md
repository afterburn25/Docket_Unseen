# Jennings 8 — Current Status

## Official public status

### Classification caution

The phrase "Jennings 8 murders" is widely used, and the 2009 task force said it was treating all eight as murders unless proven otherwise. However, KPLC reported in 2019 that Sheriff Ivy Woods said not all eight had officially been ruled homicides and that there was nothing official proving the deaths were all connected or caused by one serial killer.

Docket Unseen should therefore use precise victim-by-victim cause/manner language and avoid implying that a homicide ruling exists where the coroner record remains undetermined.



As of 2026:
- Jefferson Davis Parish Sheriff's Office still maintains Hot Case pages for all eight women.
- No conviction has resolved any of the eight deaths.
- Sheriff Kyle C. Miers is the current Jefferson Davis Parish sheriff; Louisiana Sheriffs' Association lists his assumption of office as 2024.

## Reported 2024–2026 re-review

An August 2026 Bayou Justice report says Sheriff Kyle Miers told the Jennings Daily News:
- his administration inherited the eight cases in July 2024;
- detectives chose to restart/review from the beginning rather than simply continue previous work;
- by July 1, 2026 they had dedicated approximately **3,800 hours**;
- they had generated more than **1,300 pages** of documentation;
- Miers said his office remained committed to pursuing justice.

Source:
https://www.bayoujustice.com/p/three-thousand-eight-hundred-hours

Important source note:
This is a **secondary report quoting a Jennings Daily News interview**. Docket Unseen should obtain the original Jennings Daily News article or direct JDPSO confirmation before presenting the exact numbers as primary fact.

Do not adopt the article author's interpretation that restarting from the beginning proves the current office distrusted earlier investigators; the author explicitly identifies that interpretation as his own.
