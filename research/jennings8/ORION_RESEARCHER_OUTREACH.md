# Draft researcher inquiry

Subject:
Jennings / Jeff Davis 8 Orion analysis — follow-up research question

Body:

Hello Dr. [Helbich/Leitner],

I am researching a documentary project concerning the unresolved Jeff Davis 8 / Jennings 8 cases in Louisiana.

I have been reviewing your work:

- "Data Mining of Collaboratively Collected Geographic Crime Information Using an Unsupervised Neural Network Approach" (AutoCarto 2012), and
- "Exploration of unstructured narrative crime reports: An unsupervised neural network and point pattern analysis approach" (2013).

The 2012 paper states that the 172 Information Packages associated with Necole Guillory were used as a pilot and that, if useful to the task force, the analysis was planned to be rerun on the entire Orion dataset.

The 2013 paper reports that the pilot produced previously unknown relationships that the Jennings Police Task Force considered potentially important.

I am trying to determine whether any full-dataset follow-up was ever completed.

If you are able to answer without disclosing protected investigative information, could you please tell me:

1. Was the analysis ever rerun using the full Jennings / Jeff Davis 8 Orion dataset?
2. Was a task-force presentation, technical report, map set, or other nonconfidential output produced beyond the published papers?
3. Did the task force ever report back to the research team about whether the newly identified patterns generated useful investigative follow-up?
4. Does any anonymized supplementary material survive that could be shared for historical/documentary research?
5. Are there other researchers, publications, theses, or conference presentations I should consult?

I am not seeking victim-sensitive or confidential investigative identities. An answer limited to the history and scope of the research project would be extremely helpful.

Thank you for your time.

# Why this outreach matters

A positive answer could reveal:
- whether all eight cases were ever jointly text-mined;
- whether victim subsets/clusters were identified;
- whether investigators followed up the "previously unknown" relationships;
- whether anonymized maps or presentations still exist.

A negative answer is also important:
- it would confirm the promising Necole pilot was not expanded, despite the original plan.
