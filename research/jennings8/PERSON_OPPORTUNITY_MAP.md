# Jennings 8 — Person-Centric Opportunity & Connection Map

## Purpose

This file flips the victim matrix around.

Instead of asking:
> Who is linked to each victim?

it asks:
> For each recurring person, which victims are they actually linked to, by what evidence, and where does the connection stop?

This is not a guilt ranking.

Connection types:
- **OFFICIAL / CONTEMPORANEOUS**
- **FAMILY / WITNESS**
- **LATER INVESTIGATIVE REPORTING**
- **INSTITUTIONAL / ROLE**
- **UNVERIFIED THEORY**

---

# Frankie Richard

## Publicly documented / strongly reported victim links

### Loretta
- family witness later says Loretta entered Richard's vehicle shortly before disappearance;
- Richard acknowledged knowing Loretta;
- later reporting places him in her final-day social network.

Strength:
**FAMILY WITNESS + LATER INVESTIGATIVE REPORTING**

### Ernestine
- KPLC 2007 says Richard was being investigated for possible involvement in Ernestine and other early deaths.
- No formal public Ernestine charge against Richard.

Strength:
**OFFICIAL CROSS-CASE INVESTIGATIVE INTEREST, WEAK VICTIM-SPECIFIC PROOF**

### Kristen
- KPLC 2007 says Richard and Hannah Conner faced second-degree murder charges in Kristen's case.
- later Sheriff's Office account disputes that characterization.
- Richard remained a person of interest.

Strength:
**VERY STRONG PUBLIC INVESTIGATIVE LINK / RECORD CONTRADICTION**

### Whitnei
- KPLC says Richard had been seen with at least three of the first four women near their deaths.
- KNOE says Richard was last to see at least two of eight alive.
- Ethan Brown specifically says Richard was one of the last people seen with Whitnei.
- family-derived chronology points to Richard's house after Whitnei left home.

Strength:
**STRONG SOCIAL / LAST-CONTACT REPORTING, PRIMARY STATEMENT STILL NEEDED**

### Muggy
- Richard knew the broader social network.
- no strong victim-specific last-contact or charge currently in public dossier.

Strength:
**BROAD SOCIAL LINK ONLY**

### Crystal
- broader network connection;
- no strong public victim-specific contact currently in dossier.

Strength:
**BROAD SOCIAL LINK ONLY**

### Brittney
- PJI characterizes task-force material as linking Teresa Gary and Richard to Brittney's exploitation/trafficking.
- underlying Taskforce Report p. 46 not obtained.

Strength:
**ATTRIBUTED TASK-FORCE-DERIVED LEAD / NOT HOMICIDE PROOF**

### Necole
- Richard knew Necole and later told Brown she "knew a whole lot."
- no public last-contact or homicide charge.

Strength:
**SOCIAL / ATTRIBUTED STATEMENT**

## Overall
Richard is the broadest recurring non-law-enforcement person in the public narrative, but the **strongest victim-specific links are concentrated in Loretta, Kristen, and Whitnei**.

There is no public forensic evidence tying him to all eight.
