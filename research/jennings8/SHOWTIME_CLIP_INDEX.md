## Clip acquisition rules

If footage is used in final Docket Unseen edit:

1. locate official Paramount/Showtime master or authorized clip;
2. log exact in/out;
3. use only what is needed for documentary analysis;
4. surround disputed claims with Docket Unseen context;
5. avoid long uninterrupted excerpts;
6. log rights/license/fair-use status;
7. whenever a clip points to a court/police/ethics record, seek that underlying record.

## Principle

The Showtime series is best used as a **map to interviews and claims**, not the project's source of truth.
