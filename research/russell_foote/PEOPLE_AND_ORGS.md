# People and Organizations — Russell Foote Research

This is a role map, not a suspect list.

## Russell "Russ" Foote
- Victim.
- Age 28 at death.
- Managed/directed the Lafayette Red Cross operation.
- Public reporting says he had been examining accounting irregularities before his death.

## Kevin Foote
- Russell Foote's son.
- Was seven when his father was killed.
- Journalist/sports broadcaster who later conducted extensive research into the case.
- Important participant source, but his investigative conclusions still require independent corroboration.

## Guy Barnett
- Identified in 2025 reporting as one of the original detectives.
- Publicly stated his view of motive and the strength/limitations of the circumstantial case.
- His statements are evidence of the investigator's recollection/opinion, not a judicial finding.

## Lafayette Parish Sheriff's Office (LPSO)
- Original/current investigating agency identified by public sources.
- Primary target for the initial report, case identifiers, releasable administrative records, and evidence-retention history.

## Lafayette Red Cross / American Red Cross
- Russell Foote's employer.
- Potential holder or archival source for personnel, audit, financial-control, correspondence, fire-loss, insurance, and chapter-administration records.

## Lafayette fire / municipal authorities
- Potential custodian of April 1974 fire-call, incident, building, and arson-administration records.

## Guaranty Bank
- Bank referenced in family/reporter accounts regarding Red Cross financial records.
- Historical private banking records may be unavailable or restricted; archival references, court exhibits, or copies retained elsewhere may be more realistic targets.

## `FORMER_RED_CROSS_SECRETARY`
- An uncharged former employee discussed in public reporting as part of the historical investigation.
- KPLC deliberately did not name her in its 2025 report because she was never charged in Russell Foote's death.
- A family-authored investigative account does name her, but this research repository will use a role label until primary records justify including the name for a specific documentary purpose.
- A grand jury reportedly declined to indict her in the homicide.
- **Presumed innocent of the homicide.**

## District attorney / grand jury
- Public reporting says the homicide was presented to a grand jury and no indictment resulted.
- Grand-jury testimony and deliberations are generally secret; research should focus on lawfully public administrative/docket material and contemporaneous reporting, not secret proceedings.

## Historical media
- The Daily Advertiser
- KLFY
- KPLC
- KATC and other local archives as applicable

These organizations may hold contemporaneous reporting, photographs, video, or references to records that no longer exist in government files.
