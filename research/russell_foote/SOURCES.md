# Source Ledger — Russell Foote

## Reliability Key

- **A — Primary / official:** original government record, court record, contemporaneous document, direct archival material.
- **B — Strong secondary:** reputable reporting with named interviews and/or document references.
- **C — Participant/family investigative account:** valuable first-hand context and leads; independently verify material claims.
- **D — Derivative / aggregator:** useful for discovery, not a preferred final authority.
