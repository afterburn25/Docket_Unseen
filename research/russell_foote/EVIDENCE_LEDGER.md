## Chain-of-Custody Questions

For every physical item we locate, record:

- collection date/time;
- collector;
- exact scene location;
- evidence number;
- storage location;
- transfers;
- testing performed;
- test date and analyst/lab;
- current retention status;
- destruction date and authority, if destroyed;
- whether photographs, reports, latent prints, ballistic measurements, or derived laboratory data survive after the physical item itself was destroyed.

## Modern-Testing Questions

Do **not** assume biological material survives. Determine first whether any of the following exist:

- retained vehicle material;
- cartridge cases/projectiles;
- clothing;
- fingerprints/latent-print cards or photographs;
- blood/biological swabs;
- envelopes, paper, or other touch-DNA-capable items;
- original negatives or high-resolution scene photographs;
- ballistic comparison measurements;
- pathology slides or retained tissue.

Any proposal for modern testing should go through law enforcement or an authorized forensic custodian.
