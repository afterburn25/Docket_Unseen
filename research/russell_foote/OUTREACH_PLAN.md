# Outreach Plan — Russell Foote

No outreach has been sent from this project yet.

The goal is to obtain records and cooperation without accusing anyone, pressuring witnesses, or interfering with the open homicide investigation.

## 1. Lafayette Parish Sheriff's Office

**Purpose**
- initial homicide report;
- incident/case number;
- evidence/property inventory where public;
- evidence-retention/destruction documentation;
- current cold-case contact/tip procedure.

**Tone**
Historical research, evidence preservation, and public-record access. Do not ask the records custodian to endorse a theory.

## 2. Lafayette Fire Department

**Purpose**
- April 9, 1974 initial fire report;
- April 26, 1974 initial fire report;
- incident numbers;
- present custodian of any historical investigative file;
- retention/disposition information if no file survives.

**Tone**
Ask first what records exist. Do not state that either fire was arson unless a primary record classifies it that way.

## 3. Kevin Foote / Russell Foote family

**Highest-value questions**
1. What exact records did the family receive from LPSO?
2. Is there a scanned/digital copy that can be reviewed for research?
3. Was there a court petition/order connected to access?
4. Does the family possess the May 1974 interview transcripts referenced in later writing?
5. Does the family possess copies of the 1974 fire/arson records or KLFY broadcast?
6. Which physical evidence items were they told had been destroyed, and by whom?
7. Are there photographs, letters, Red Cross records, or family notes that can be preserved digitally?
8. Would the family consider designating a researcher under the applicable Louisiana access process if additional evidence inspection became useful?

**Safeguard**
Make clear that Docket Unseen will distinguish evidence from theories and will not present an uncharged person as guilty.

## 5. Lafayette Public Library / Advantage Archives

**Purpose**
Use the free digitized Daily Advertiser archive before requesting staff research.

Archive:
https://lafayette.advantage-preservation.com/

If the archive search does not surface a date known to exist, ask whether:
- OCR can miss names;
- a specific issue/date can be browsed manually;
- higher-resolution scans/PDFs are available;
- there are gaps in 1974 holdings.

## 6. KLFY

**Purpose**
Locate the reported November 1974 broadcast concerning the Red Cross fire/financial investigation/Foote homicide.

Ask for:
- archive index/air date;
- script;
- transcript;
- audio/film/video if retained;
- rights/licensing contact.

Do not ask the station to identify a confidential source from a 1974 report.

## 7. American Red Cross

**Purpose**
Determine whether national/regional archives retain:
- Lafayette chapter audit records;
- 1973–1974 financial-control correspondence;
- Russell Foote personnel/management records that can lawfully be shared;
- fire/property-loss records;
- insurance correspondence;
- records of any federal/Department of Defense-related audit described in later accounts.

## Contact-log fields

- recipient;
- exact request;
- response;
- documents promised;
- documents received;
- restrictions/permissions;
- follow-up date;
