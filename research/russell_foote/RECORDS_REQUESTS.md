# Records Acquisition Plan — Russell Foote

## 1. Lafayette Parish Sheriff's Office — Initial Homicide Report

**Current request channel:** recordsrequest@lafayettesheriff.com  
**Official records page:** https://lafayettesheriff.com/divisions/administrative-services/records/

Louisiana R.S. 44:3 currently provides that the initial report of the officer(s) investigating a complaint is a public record, although broader investigative records may be exempt while litigation is pending or reasonably anticipated.

### Draft request

> Subject: Public Records Request — Russell Foote homicide, May 17, 1974
>
> Pursuant to the Louisiana Public Records Law, I request an electronic copy of the initial report of the officer or officers investigating the May 17, 1974 homicide of Russell "Russ" Foote in Lafayette Parish.
>
> I also request, to the extent each item is public and can be segregated from exempt investigative material:
>
> 1. the original incident/offense number and any later cold-case identifier;
> 2. the non-exempt property/evidence inventory associated with the initial report;
> 3. records reflecting the present retention status of physical evidence;
> 4. any administrative record identifying the date, authority, or retention policy under which evidence from the case was destroyed or disposed of;
> 5. any publicly releasable crime-scene diagram, dispatch chronology, or incident-location record.
>
> If any requested material is withheld, please provide the statutory basis for withholding and release any reasonably segregable non-exempt portion. Electronic production is preferred.
>
> This request is for historical research concerning an unsolved homicide and is not intended to interfere with any active investigation.

## 2. Lafayette Consolidated Government / Fire Records

**Official portal:** https://www.lafayettela.gov/your-government/public-records-request/

### Target request
Request records for fires at the Lafayette Red Cross office / War Memorial Building on or about:
- April 9, 1974;
- April 26, 1974.

Ask for:
- incident/fire reports;
- dispatch logs;
- cause-and-origin findings;
- arson-investigation administrative records/indexes;
- scene photographs;
- building inspection/repair records;
- correspondence regarding transfer of the matter to another agency;
- record-retention/destruction documentation if the incident file no longer exists.

### Important phrasing
If no responsive file exists, ask the custodian to identify:
- whether records are known to have been destroyed;
- applicable retention schedule;
- destruction date if documented;
- whether records were transferred to another custodian.

## 3. Louisiana State Fire Marshal / Historical State Fire Records

First determine whether the state agency participated in either April 1974 fire investigation. Do not assume involvement.

If confirmed, request:
- case/index number;
- public incident summary;
- administrative disposition;
- photographs/reports that are lawfully releasable;
- retention/disposition records if the investigative file no longer exists.

## 4. Lafayette Parish Clerk / District Attorney

Goal: establish **public administrative history**, not secret grand-jury testimony.

Ask whether any public docket, minute, index, bill, or no-bill record is maintained that can establish the date/term associated with the reported grand-jury consideration of Russell Foote's homicide or related financial offenses.

Do not request:
- secret grand-jury testimony;
- juror deliberations;
- protected witness information.

## 5. American Red Cross

This is a voluntary archival request, not a Louisiana public-records request.

Targets:
- Lafayette chapter audit/financial-control records, 1973–1974;
- correspondence concerning Russell Foote;
- records regarding April 1974 fires;
- insurance/property-loss documentation;
- personnel/administrative material that can lawfully be released;
- any audit connected with Department of Defense-related activity described in later family reporting.

## 6. Newspaper / Broadcast Archives

Acquire:
- Daily Advertiser, May 17–June 1974 and November 1974;
- complete May 2018 "Unforgotten" / Russell Foote series;
- KLFY 1974 archive references if surviving;
- photographs, captions, and contemporaneous descriptions.

Potential repositories:
- local newspaper archive;
- University of Louisiana at Lafayette libraries/microfilm;
- station archives;
- Internet/archive databases;
- family-held clippings.

## 7. Coroner / Vital Records

Louisiana law restricts access to post-mortem forensic reports in important ways. Family members may have access rights not available to the general public.

Research route:
- obtain death certificate through lawful public/family channels;
- ask the family whether they already possess the post-mortem report;
- do not represent that a general public requester is automatically entitled to a confidential forensic report.
