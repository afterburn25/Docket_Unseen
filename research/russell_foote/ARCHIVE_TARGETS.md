### Acquisition priority

Obtain complete copies from one or more of:
- Daily Advertiser/Gannett archive or licensing desk;
- newspapers.com / ProQuest / NewsBank access if available through a library;
- University of Louisiana at Lafayette library;

- Internet Archive snapshots where lawful and complete.

Do not rely on search-engine snippets as substitutes for the articles.

## 1974 newspaper windows

Search complete issues for:
- April 8–10, 1974 — reported first Red Cross fire.
- April 24–28, 1974 — employment separation and reported major Red Cross/War Memorial Building fire.
- May 15–24, 1974 — bank/audit events, homicide, funeral, early investigation.
- June–December 1974 — embezzlement/arson/homicide investigation and reported grand-jury activity.
- November 1974 specifically — reported KLFY item linking the arson, embezzlement, and homicide as an investigative theory.

For each item capture:
- newspaper/date/page;
- headline/byline;
- full-page image/PDF if licensed;
- exact wording of law-enforcement claims;
- names/titles as printed at the time;
- any photographs or maps;
- corrections/follow-ups.
