# Scene / Geospatial Research — Russell Foote

## Current scene descriptions

We do not yet have a verified scene pin.

Current secondary sources describe the location in slightly different ways:

- KPLC (2025): along what is now West Congress Street.
- KPEL (2018): in the middle of the then-two-lane West Congress roadway; the article adds "(At the time, it was Route 1)."
- Project: Cold Case: near Guilbeau Street.

These descriptions are close enough to justify focused map research, but not precise enough to publish a definitive location.

## Map request draft

> Subject: Digitization/research request — 1973 Lafayette city map
>
> I am conducting historical research concerning the May 17, 1974 unsolved homicide of Russell Foote in Lafayette. I am trying to verify the historical configuration and street/route designation of West Congress Street near Guilbeau Road at the time.
>
> Your Louisiana Maps index lists the following item in Map Case Drawer 7:
>
> "1973 Lafayette: A City Map. Sun Star Map Service for Greater Lafayette Chamber of Commerce. LaRm G 4014 .L3 1973 .L3."
>
> Would it be possible to obtain a research-quality digital image or scan of the portion showing West Congress Street and Guilbeau Road, or information on the procedure/cost for a digitization request?
>
> If the 1970 Lafayette Base Map or 1975 city map provides clearer road designations for the same area, I would also appreciate guidance on those holdings.
>
> This is historical research; the goal is to accurately reconstruct the 1974 roadway and avoid projecting modern street configurations onto the crime scene.

## Questions the maps should answer

1. Exact West Congress alignment in 1973–74.
2. Whether Guilbeau Road intersected West Congress in its present configuration.
3. Road width / divided vs. undivided status.
4. Whether "Route 1" appears anywhere as an official or colloquial designation.
5. Nearby subdivisions, structures, businesses, vacant land, drainage/coulees, and access roads.
6. Whether later road widening changed the likely scene.
7. Candidate locations consistent with both "West Congress" and "near Guilbeau."

## Verification rule

Do not use a modern map as proof of the 1974 scene configuration.

A definitive scene pin should require at least one of:
- original LPSO scene diagram or report with a precise location;
- contemporaneous newspaper map/photo/caption;
- historical road map plus a sufficiently precise original location description.

## "Route 1" caution

KPEL's 2018 article says West Congress was "Route 1" at the time. That wording has not yet been verified against a 1973–74 map or official road record.

Until verified, record it as:
**REPORTED HISTORICAL ROAD DESIGNATION — UNCONFIRMED.**
