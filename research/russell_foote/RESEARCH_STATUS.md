Do not convert this research into an accusatory episode or thumbnail while the central primary-record gaps remain open.

A Docket Unseen episode can eventually discuss:
- the unsolved homicide;
- the documented investigation;
- the family's cover-up concerns as attributed allegations;
- missing/destroyed records where independently verified;
- what the official record does and does not establish.

It should not declare an uncharged person guilty or state that a cover-up occurred without strong documentary support.
